Bowlero at Tysons Galleria will become Lucky Strike Entertainment in early February, bringing a new name and look to the venue without closing its bowling lanes.

The change is part of a company-wide rebranding that began when the former Bowlero Corporation officially adopted the Lucky Strike Entertainment name on Dec. 12.

A company spokesperson said no major renovations are planned. Updates will include new signs, fresh paint, and changes to portions of the interior and exterior.

The venue’s existing menu will remain, including Baja Shrimp Tacos, Katsu Chicken Bao Buns, Mike’s Hot Honey Chicken Sandwich, salads and bowls.

The 39,000-square-foot Tysons Galleria location opened in fall 2021 with dozens of bowling lanes, more than 70 arcade games and a bar.

The company operates more than 350 locations across the United States, Canada and Mexico, including venues in Annandale and Arlington. It plans to convert 75 Bowlero locations nationwide to the Lucky Strike brand over the next two years.

The company said the new identity is intended to feel approachable and timeless while retaining an upscale image.
