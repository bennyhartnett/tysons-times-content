Winter Restaurant Week returns to the Washington area Jan. 19-25, bringing discounted three-course meals to dozens of restaurants across Fairfax County.

The biannual promotion could offer a welcome lift for the region’s food service industry following a difficult year marked by inflation, tariffs and other pressures.

Participating restaurants will serve curated menus for lunch, brunch and dinner. Lunch and brunch will cost $25 or $35 per person, while dinner options will be priced at $40, $55 or $65.

Menus are available through the Restaurant Association of Metropolitan Washington’s website.
