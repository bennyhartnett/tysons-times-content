Winter Restaurant Week returns Jan. 19-25, 2026, with specially priced three-course meals at restaurants across Tysons, Reston and nearby Northern Virginia communities.

Dinner menus are available at $40, $55 or $65, while brunch and lunch options cost $25 or $35. Some restaurants also offer wine or cocktail pairings, outdoor seating and delivery, with availability varying by location.

The Restaurant Association Metropolitan Washington presents Metropolitan Washington Restaurant Week twice each year.

- **Agora Tysons**, 7911 Westpark Drive, Tysons: $35 brunch, $35 lunch and $65 dinner. Wine or cocktail pairings are offered.
