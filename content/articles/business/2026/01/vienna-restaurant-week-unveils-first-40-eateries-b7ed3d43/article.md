Vienna Restaurant Week will return for its fifth year with 10 days of dining specials at restaurants across town.

Running from Feb. 27 through March 8, the event will feature specially priced prix fixe menus, exclusive offers and limited-time dining experiences. The first 40 participating eateries have now been announced, with more restaurants and menu details expected in the coming weeks.

The initial lineup spans a wide range of cuisines, including Indian, Mediterranean, French, Lebanese, Peruvian, Chinese and American food.

The Town of Vienna Economic Development Department hosts the annual event to support local businesses and encourage residents to explore the town’s dining scene.
