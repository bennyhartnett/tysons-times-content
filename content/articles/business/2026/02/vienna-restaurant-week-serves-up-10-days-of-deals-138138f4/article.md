Vienna Restaurant Week returns Feb. 27 through March 8, bringing exclusive discounts, prix fixe menus and limited-time specials to more than 40 local restaurants and businesses.

The 10-day event will feature options for a range of tastes and budgets, from breakfast stops and sweet treats to international cuisine and fine dining. Participating businesses are creating offers specifically for the occasion.

Vienna Economic Development Director Nicole Toulouse said the event celebrates the town’s restaurants, their employees and the small businesses that contribute to Vienna’s character. She said several recent restaurant openings give Northern Virginia residents another reason to explore the town’s dining scene.

Presented by the Town of Vienna Economic Development Department, the annual event is intended to support local businesses, strengthen the local economy and raise regional awareness of Vienna’s diverse food offerings.

Participating restaurants and menus are available on the Explore Vienna website.
