Arianna Wallace serves as senior vice president of marketing at Certified Title Corporation, supporting title services in Virginia, Maryland, Washington, D.C., West Virginia and Florida.

Wallace began her real estate career in title processing, where she worked with property records, lien searches and closing documents. Her responsibilities grew to include client service, marketing strategy and senior leadership.

Her work now covers title searches and examinations, title insurance coordination, document preparation and closings involving buyers, sellers, lenders and real estate agents. She also supports mobile and remote closing options for clients unable to attend a traditional settlement.

Wallace works with real estate agents, loan officers and brokers and helps buyers understand title insurance and the closing process. Her approach combines the technical requirements of property transactions with an emphasis on communication among everyone involved.
