Laurie Mensing has spent more than two decades building a real estate career in Northern Virginia, becoming a high-volume agent in McLean and ranking among the top 1% of agents nationwide.

Based at Long & Foster’s McLean office, Mensing works throughout Fairfax and Arlington counties and nearby communities. She has repeatedly been recognized as a leading company agent in the Mid-Atlantic region and has held a leadership role within the firm’s Forbes Global Properties network.

Her business focuses on negotiation, property preparation and luxury-home marketing. Listings are promoted through professional photography, digital campaigns and channels intended to reach local, national and international buyers.

Mensing also connects clients with lenders, inspectors, contractors and relocation specialists. Her process includes market analysis, staging guidance, pricing strategy, targeted promotion, negotiation and hands-on assistance through closing.

Her industry recognition includes appearances in national and regional rankings from REAL Trends, Washingtonian and Northern Virginia Magazine. She has also received repeated company honors for sales production.

Mensing’s portfolio ranges from suburban estates and luxury properties to homes suited to commuters and relocating families. Her work is concentrated in McLean, Arlington, Fairfax and surrounding Northern Virginia communities.
