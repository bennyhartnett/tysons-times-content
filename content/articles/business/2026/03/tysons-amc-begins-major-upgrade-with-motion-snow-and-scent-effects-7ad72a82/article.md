A months-long renovation is getting underway at AMC Theatres in Tysons Corner Center, bringing upgraded seating, refreshed common areas and new premium screenings designed to make movies a more immersive experience.

A temporary wall has already been installed around the box office, with construction expected to begin early this week. AMC spokesperson Cassie Kennard said the project will update the box office, most auditorium seating, hallways and MacGuffins Bar.

The 16-screen theater will also gain a second Dolby Cinema auditorium, its first Prime at AMC screen and a 4DX auditorium featuring motion-based seats and more than 21 effects synchronized with the action on screen. Those effects can include wind, water, snow, vibration and scents.

AMC began introducing 4DX at U.S. theaters after announcing last summer that it planned to install 40 screens by the end of 2027. The format debuted at AMC Gulf Pointe in Houston in February and is also listed as coming soon to theaters in Denver, Kansas City and Atlanta.

The experience will cost more than a standard screening. Although the usual surcharge remains unclear, a Sunday afternoon showing of Pixar’s “Hoppers” at the Houston theater costs about $9 in a regular auditorium and $16 in 4DX.

AMC compares the format to a theme park attraction and does not recommend it for every moviegoer. Patrons must be at least 4 years old and 3½ feet tall.

Prime at AMC will offer more conventional upgrades, including plush reclining seats, laser projection and enhanced surround sound. AMC Signature Recliners will also be installed in every other auditorium except the 4DX and IMAX rooms.

The theater opened in September 2005 as part of an expansion of Tysons Corner Center. Its previous major additions were an IMAX auditorium in 2010 and the later conversion of another screen to Dolby Cinema. Neither existing premium auditorium will be affected by the renovation.

AMC plans to keep the theater open while work proceeds in phases. Kennard said the project is expected to be substantially complete by early fall, with photos of the improvements to be released later this year.
