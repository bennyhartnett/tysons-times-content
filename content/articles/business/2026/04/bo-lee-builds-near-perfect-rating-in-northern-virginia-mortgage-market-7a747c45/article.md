Bo Lee, a loan officer and branch leader at Movement Mortgage, has earned a 4.98 out of 5 rating across 95 verified reviews while serving homebuyers throughout Northern Virginia and beyond.

Based in Manassas, Lee works with clients in Fairfax, Tysons Corner and the broader region. She is licensed in Virginia, Maryland, Washington, D.C., and Texas.

Her financing options include conventional, FHA, VA, USDA and jumbo loans, along with refinancing, reverse mortgages, home equity lines of credit and down payment assistance programs. She also handles FHA 203(k) renovation loans, VA streamline refinances, investment-property financing and specialized programs for medical professionals.

Movement Mortgage uses upfront underwriting and promotes a seven-day processing system designed to help accelerate closing schedules when requested by real estate agents. The company identifies itself as an Impact Lender, committing at least 10% of its profits to community initiatives and reporting that it returns nearly half of its profits for that purpose.

Clients reviewing Lee frequently highlight her responsiveness, mortgage knowledge and ability to explain the process. One first-time buyer said she provided extensive education, helped secure a favorable rate and quickly arranged a VA appraisal. Another client reported completing a closing in 10 days with little difficulty reaching final approval.

Lee is also a preferred partner of NOVA Real Producers Magazine, a designation for professionals vetted by participating real estate agents in the region. Her role as branch leader includes both mortgage production and support for her team.
