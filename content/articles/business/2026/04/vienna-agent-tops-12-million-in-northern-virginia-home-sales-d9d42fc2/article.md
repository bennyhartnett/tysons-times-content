Missy Whittington has sold more than $12 million in homes during her first few years in Northern Virginia real estate, drawing on an earlier career in accounting and finance to guide buyers and sellers through high-value transactions.

Whittington works from Samson Properties’ Vienna office at 361 Maple Avenue West and serves clients across Vienna and the broader Northern Virginia market. Her financial background helps her evaluate property values, negotiate terms and explain the monetary implications of a sale or purchase.

She attributes her approach to the work ethic she learned from her parents and the discipline developed while playing youth softball. Her client philosophy emphasizes personal attention and the emotional as well as financial significance of real estate decisions.

Whittington is part of The Casey Samson Team. The group reported $231 million in production during 2025 and said 97% of its homes received offers above the asking price within their first week, compared with a reported regional rate of 25%.

The team ranked first in Vienna for transaction sides and sales volume. It was also listed at No. 504 nationally for sides and No. 60 for volume, as well as No. 17 in Virginia for sides and No. 4 for volume. In 2026, the team reported that all its listings had either sold or were pending, compared with a broader-market rate of 36%.

Whittington’s service area includes a market where the average home price exceeds $1.6 million. Through Samson Properties and the team, she has access to Bright MLS data, marketing systems and collaborative support for negotiations.
