Allie Chamberlain, a Samson Properties real estate agent serving Northern Virginia, reports a 99.4% list-to-sale price ratio and an average of 10 days on the market for her listings.

Chamberlain has worked in real estate for more than 13 years and holds the Accredited Buyer’s Representative designation. She is also an Northern Virginia Association of Realtors top producer and a member of the Real Estate Buyers Agent Council.

Before entering real estate, Chamberlain spent more than a decade managing large corporate hospitality conferences and events. She says that experience helped her develop the contract and negotiation skills she now uses when representing property buyers and sellers.

A Northern Virginia resident since 1993, Chamberlain lived in Vienna beginning in 2003 and more recently in Oakton. Her community roles have included PTA president at a Vienna elementary school, vice president of the Greater Tysons Green Civic Association and treasurer of the Fairfax Chapter of the Daughters of the American Revolution. She has also volunteered for more than 90 hours with the Young Men’s Service League.

Her services include residential sales, buyer representation, investment-property transactions, staging consultations, market analysis, pricing strategy, contract negotiation, relocation assistance and home valuations.

Chamberlain primarily works in Vienna and Oakton, with additional transactions across McLean, Reston, Arlington and the broader Fairfax County area. Sales cited in her professional profile range from a $950,000 Vienna property to a $1.67 million McLean home. Other listed transactions include homes in Vienna and Oakton that sold for between $1.035 million and $1.6 million.

Chamberlain describes helping buyers find homes and preparing sellers’ properties for the market as the most rewarding parts of her work. Client testimonials included in her profile emphasize her responsiveness, market knowledge and assistance with negotiations and deadlines.
