Clarity plans to open its second restaurant at 11 a.m. July 24 at Reston Town Center, following permitting delays that pushed the debut back from spring.

The restaurant is at 1996 Opportunity Way in the town center’s expansion area. Its 4,500-square-foot space can accommodate about 175 guests indoors, with room for another 40 on the patio.

Owner Viswanath Vasireddy said Reston’s growth potential influenced the decision to establish Clarity’s second location in the community.

“From the very beginning, our goal has been to create a place where guests feel at home and every visit is memorable,” Vasireddy said.

The Reston restaurant plans to serve lunch and dinner, along with happy hour and takeout. The location also expects to host private events.

Its menu is expected to mix dishes already served at Clarity’s Vienna location with new offerings. A six-course tasting menu is also planned.

The planned July opening comes later than initially anticipated. Clarity had been expected to open the Reston restaurant during spring 2026, but permitting issues delayed the schedule.

Clarity may not be Vasireddy’s only new restaurant at Reston Town Center this year. He was also planning to open a Dogfish Head Alehouse there later in 2026, although a specific opening date was not provided.
