Red Door Metro, a McLean-based real estate team founded by George M. Mrad, serves buyers and sellers across Northern Virginia, including McLean, Vienna and Arlington.

Mrad has more than two decades of real estate experience. His team works with first-time buyers, families, professionals relocating to the Washington area and owners of multimillion-dollar properties.

The company provides pricing guidance, market analysis, professional photography, property marketing, transaction coordination and offer negotiations. Its approach emphasizes communication and long-term client relationships, with repeat customers and referrals accounting for a significant part of its business.

Red Door Metro also draws on neighborhood-level knowledge, including school districts, commuting patterns and local pricing trends, to guide clients through purchases and sales.

The company is located at 1775 Tysons Blvd., Suite 1450, in McLean.
