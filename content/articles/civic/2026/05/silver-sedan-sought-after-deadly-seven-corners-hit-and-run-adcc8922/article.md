Police are searching for the driver of a silver sedan after a pedestrian was struck and killed early Tuesday morning in the Seven Corners area of Fairfax County.

The crash happened May 26, 2026, near the intersection of Arlington Boulevard and Patrick Henry Drive. Investigators said the man was walking in the area when the sedan’s driver hit him and left the scene.

Emergency responders took the injured pedestrian to a hospital. He was later pronounced dead.

Authorities identified the vehicle involved only as a silver sedan. The archived report did not provide the man’s identity, a more detailed description of the car or information about the direction in which the driver fled.

Fairfax County police are asking anyone who witnessed the crash or has other information about the driver or vehicle to contact the department as the hit-and-run investigation continues.
