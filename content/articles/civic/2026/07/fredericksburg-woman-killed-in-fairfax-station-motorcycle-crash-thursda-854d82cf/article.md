A 39-year-old Fredericksburg woman died Thursday after her motorcycle collided with an SUV at a Fairfax Station intersection, according to Fairfax County police.

Jennifer Hollingsworth was riding a 2025 Suzuki GSXR 750 northbound on Ox Road around 7:13 p.m. when she struck a 2021 Buick Enclave turning left from southbound Ox Road onto eastbound Chapel Road at the Chapel Road intersection. She was pronounced dead at the scene.

The Buick driver was not injured and stayed at the scene. Police said investigators do not believe alcohol was a factor for the SUV driver, though the Crash Reconstruction Unit is still examining whether speed or alcohol contributed to the collision on Hollingsworth's part.

Anyone with information about the crash can contact Fairfax County Police at 703-280-0543 or submit an anonymous tip through Crime Solvers at 1-866-411-TIPS.
