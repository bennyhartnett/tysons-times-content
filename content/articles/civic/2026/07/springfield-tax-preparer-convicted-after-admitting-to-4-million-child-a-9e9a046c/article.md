A Springfield man who worked as a tax preparer and museum guide has been convicted on child sexual abuse material charges after admitting to possessing approximately 4 million illicit images.

Nathaniel Lee, 39, was found guilty Monday following a bench trial on three counts related to his possession of the material. He is scheduled to be sentenced on Jan. 6.

## Investigation Began in 2022

The case against Lee originated in December 2022 when police discovered he was requesting child sex abuse images through Freenet, a file-sharing network.

Authorities executed a search warrant at Lee's residence in June 2024, seizing multiple electronic devices. Investigators found what prosecutors described as a "large volume" of images and videos depicting minors engaged in sexually explicit conduct.

During the investigation, Lee admitted to possessing around 4 million images of child sexual abuse material, according to prosecutors.

Lee had been working as a tax preparer and served as a guide at the Franconia Museum prior to his arrest.
