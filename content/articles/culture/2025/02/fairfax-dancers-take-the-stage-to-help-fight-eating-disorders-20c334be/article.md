The Fairfax Academy Dancers will turn their annual performance into a benefit for people battling eating disorders when “Move to Mend” takes the stage Friday, Feb. 21.

The concert begins at 7 p.m. in the Fairfax High auditorium. Admission is free, with donations accepted for Hope to Heal, a charity supporting people in need who are fighting a range of eating disorders.

Students from the Fairfax Academy dance program will perform alongside alumni and community artists. The lineup includes Greater Washington Chinese Dance, Center Stage, DanceWorks at Mason, Ballet Embody, 1 PICK, Ash & Elm Dance Company, Helping Hearts Through the Arts, Rhythm and Arts Center, and the West Potomac Academy Dance program.

The Fairfax Academy for Communications and the Arts is sponsoring the event. More information and donation details are available from Fairfax Academy dance director Meredith Barnes at mbarnes@fcps.edu.
