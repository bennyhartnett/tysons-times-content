Falls Church Arts will spotlight work by emerging and established regional artists during a two-part All-Member Show running from June 7 through Aug. 10.

The first exhibit, featuring 62 artists whose last names begin with A through L, will be displayed from June 7 to July 6. A free public reception for those artists is scheduled from 7 to 9 p.m. Saturday, June 7.

Work by members with last names beginning with M through Z will follow from July 12 to Aug. 10.

The exhibits will feature artists from Virginia, Washington, D.C., and Maryland working in watercolor, acrylic, pastel, fused glass, oil, photography and sculpture, along with numerous mixed-media and printmaking techniques.

Visitors may vote for the People’s Choice Award in the A-L exhibit through June 20. The winner will be announced June 21.

The A-L participants are Brian Aburano, Jean-Marie Ahanda, John Allen, Kaveh-Michael Amiri, John Ballou, Cheryl Bearss, Blanca Belisario, Barbara Bird, Elizabeth Blakeslee, Susan Boggs, Diana Bozza, Vincent Brandi, Noreen Brunini, Maureen Budetti, Donna Byrne, Bonn Carpenter, Deborah Conn, Gretchen D’Amore, Spoorti Dandridge, Marina DiCarlo, Barbara Dixon, Vicky Dorn-Fontana, Erin Endean, Scott Engdahl, Monique Eskin, Merete Evans, Gregory Felder, Steven Ferri, Marcia Finnerty, Bill Firestone, Mara Flynn and Nick Gatz.

Also participating are Marilyn Geldzahler, Maureen Goss, James Hengst, Dan Hildt, Susan Hillson, Robert Hoff, Veronica Howard, Pamela Huffman, Gwen Infante, Lizzy Javier, Roxanne Kaylor, Phillip Kemelor, MD Tayabuzzaman Khan, Maria Kinnane, Julie Krachman, Al Krachman, Zoe Kuo, Victoria Lakes, Pamela Lakes, Gerda Lane, Jean Lauzon, Claire Lee, Jessica Lehman, Richard Levine, Trudy Levy, Naomi Lipsky, Xianling Liu, Randa Fakes LoGerfo, Mary Ludden and Xixi Luo.

The Falls Church Arts gallery is located at 700-B West Broad St. in Falls Church. Admission is free. Gallery hours are 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday and 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. Sunday.

All exhibited pieces will be available for purchase at the gallery and online.
