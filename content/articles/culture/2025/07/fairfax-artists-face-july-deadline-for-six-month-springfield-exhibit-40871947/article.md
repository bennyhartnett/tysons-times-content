ArtsFairfax is accepting applications from Fairfax County artists for an upcoming exhibition at Springfield Town Center.

The exhibit is scheduled to remain on display for six months, beginning in October 2025 and continuing through March 2026. Eligible artists who want their work considered must submit an application by July 31, 2025.

The call gives local artists an opportunity to have their work shown at the Springfield shopping center during the fall and winter and into early spring. Participation is limited to artists from Fairfax County.

Artists interested in applying should review the Springfield Town Center exhibit information provided by ArtsFairfax before the deadline. The announcement did not specify the number of artists or works that will be selected, the types of art being sought, or when applicants will learn whether they have been chosen.
