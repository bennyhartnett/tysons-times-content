Volunteer groups supporting Fairfax County’s public libraries donated more than $369,660 and contributed 37,297 hours of service during fiscal year 2025.

The figures were highlighted ahead of National Friends of the Library Week, observed Oct. 19-25, 2025. Friends groups support individual branches and the wider library system by promoting activities, assisting with community programs and raising money.

Many groups organize used-book sales and return the proceeds to the library. Their contributions help purchase books and equipment while funding programs and exhibits beyond the operating budget.

## Volunteers honored for outstanding service

The Library Board of Trustees and community members gathered at Chantilly Regional Library on Wednesday, Oct. 8, for the 2025 Friends’ Choice Awards. The event recognized Friends group members nominated by their peers for exceptional service.

This year’s honorees were:

- Karrin Lukacs, Friends of Burke Centre Library
- Caitlyn Largent, Morgan Mayernik, Tiffany Tran and Susan Vogel, Friends of Centreville Regional Library
- Liz Critchley, Brian Critchley, Helena Ferret and Barbara Levermann, Friends of Chantilly Regional Library
- Peggy Greenwood and Jack Greenwood, Friends of Culmore Library
- Jennifer Lyons and Jessica Jeffcoat Sanchez, Friends of Kingstowne Library
- Susan Gantz, Friends of Oakton Library
- Arnold Goldstein, Cindy Richards, Laura Stokowski, Phyllis Talbert and Nancy Vandel, Friends of Pohick Regional Library
- Moira Doherty, Lynda Ose, Tom Radlick, Arja Sahramaa, Marge Shapira and Ariel Welt, Friends of Reston Regional Library

- Penny Anderson, Alex Klein and Cindy Shayne Magielnicki, Friends of Tysons-Pimmit Regional Library
- Bobbie Leamer, Friends of the Virginia Room

Residents can support a Friends group through membership, volunteer involvement or donations of used books for future sales.
