Outdoor movie season is returning to Northern Virginia with family-friendly screenings planned in Vienna, Fairfax, Reston and Ashburn throughout summer 2026. Families can bring blankets or folding chairs and settle in for films under the stars.

## Big Screen on the Green

Vienna’s series begins at 6:30 p.m. Friday, May 15, with *How to Train Your Dragon* at the Vienna Town Green. Another screening is scheduled for Friday, Sept. 11, with the movie still to be announced.

## Movies in the Park

Movies will be shown at 7 p.m. Thursdays from June 18 through Aug. 20 at Strawberry Park in Mosaic, 2910 District Ave. in Fairfax. The film schedule has not yet been announced.

## Movies Under the Moon

Fairfax will host screenings at 8 p.m. on the second Friday of each month:

- June 12: *Zootopia* at Pat Rodio Park, 3800 Keith Ave.
- July 10: *The Lego Movie* at Providence Park, 10715 West Drive.
- Aug. 14: *Honey, I Shrunk the Kids* at Ratcliffe Park, 10300 Sager Ave.
- Sept. 11: *The Secret Life of Pets* at Kutner Park, 3901 Jermantown Road.

## Summerbration at Reston Station

Saturday movie events will run from 6:30 to 9 p.m. on the Green at 1800 Reston Row Plaza in Reston.

- June 6: *Rudy*
- June 13: *Back to the Future*
- June 20: *Honey, I Shrunk the Kids*
- June 27: *Big*
- July 4: *The Sandlot*
- July 11: *Cars*
- July 18: *The Bad Guys*
- July 25: *The Parent Trap*
- Aug. 1: *Minions*
- Aug. 8: *Willie Wonka and the Chocolate Factory*
- Aug. 15: *The Secret Life of Pets*
- Aug. 22: *School of Rock*
- Aug. 29: *Pitch Perfect*

## Summerbration at Loudoun Station

Loudoun Station will present the same lineup on Sundays from 6:30 to 9 p.m. at Loudoun Station District Park, 43805 Central Station Drive in Ashburn.

- June 7: *Rudy*
- June 14: *Back to the Future*
- June 21: *Honey, I Shrunk the Kids*
- June 28: *Big*
- July 5: *The Sandlot*
- July 12: *Cars*
- July 19: *The Bad Guys*
- July 26: *The Parent Trap*
- Aug. 2: *Minions*
- Aug. 9: *Willie Wonka and the Chocolate Factory*
- Aug. 16: *The Secret Life of Pets*
- Aug. 23: *School of Rock*
- Aug. 30: *Pitch Perfect*
