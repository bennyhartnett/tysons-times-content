Artmedica Aesthetics is carving out a niche in Vienna’s competitive medical-spa market by emphasizing personalized treatment plans and natural-looking cosmetic results.

The boutique practice, located at 410 Pine St. SE in downtown Vienna, offers facial rejuvenation, injectables, skin treatments and regenerative services. Its clients come from Vienna, McLean, Tysons, Great Falls and other parts of Northern Virginia.

Founder and injector Lina Colleli built the practice around subtle enhancement rather than dramatic changes. Artmedica says its goal is to help clients look refreshed and rested without appearing overdone.

The company says Colleli founded Artmedica after working in several medical-spa settings and seeing demand for a more customized, relationship-focused approach. Consultations consider a client’s facial structure, skin condition, lifestyle and personal goals before treatments are recommended.

Artmedica’s services include Botox and other wrinkle relaxers, dermal fillers, facial balancing, microneedling, chemical peels, hair restoration, fat-dissolving treatments, collagen stimulators and nonsurgical BBL procedures. The practice also offers PRF and PRF EZ Gel treatments, along with MOXI and BBL HEROic skin therapies.

Injectables and facial contouring are prominent parts of the practice. Colleli, who promotes her work online as “Injector Lina,” frequently showcases lip enhancement, profile balancing and collagen-stimulation procedures.

The clinic also highlights continuing education as injectable methods, laser technologies and regenerative treatments evolve. It says providers stay current on new techniques and technologies while prioritizing safety and individualized care.

Client testimonials featured by Artmedica commonly praise the team’s professionalism, communication and attention to detail. Customers have also highlighted thorough explanations of procedures and treatment plans, as well as results they consider natural-looking.

That restrained approach helps distinguish the practice in the crowded Vienna and Tysons aesthetics market. Instead of promoting dramatic transformations, Artmedica centers its message on rejuvenation, confidence and gradual enhancement.
