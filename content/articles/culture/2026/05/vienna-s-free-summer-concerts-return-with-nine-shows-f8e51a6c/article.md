Vienna’s Town Green will come alive with free music this summer, beginning with a 90-minute performance by the Sarah Swanner Band at 6:30 p.m. Friday, June 5.

The 2026 Summer on the Green series offers nine performances spanning country, folk, rock, blues, soul, reggae, jazz, concert band music and entertainment for children. Local and regional acts will perform throughout June, July and August.

The schedule includes:

- June 5: Sarah Swanner Band — country and folk
- June 7: Vienna Community Band — concert band
- June 17: Rocknocerous, presented by Vienna Moms Inc. — children’s music
- June 26: 2nd Sole — classic rock
- July 10: The Cassaday Concoction — rock, blues, soul and reggae
- July 24: Fat Chance — rock
- July 31: U.S. Army Blues Swamp Romp — Louisiana jazz and folk
- Aug. 7: The Maiden Band — classic rock from the 1970s to today
- Aug. 14: U.S. Navy Band Commodores — jazz

Concertgoers are encouraged to bring lawn chairs or picnic blankets. The Vienna Town Green is at 144 Maple Ave. E in Vienna.

Weather-related cancellation notices will be available by calling 703-255-7842 or checking the town’s social media account, @TownofViennaVA. The Rotary Club of Vienna is sponsoring the 2026 series.
