Summer isn't over yet. With back-to-school season approaching, August packs in a final burst of outdoor entertainment across Northern Virginia and the surrounding region, from agricultural fairs showcasing livestock and tractor demos to festivals celebrating oysters, bourbon, and watermelons.

## County Fairs Take Center Stage

The **Fairfax County Summer Carnival and Fair** kicks off July 30 through August 2 at Frying Pan Farm Park in Herndon. Families can expect carnival rides, farm animals, tractor demonstrations, and fair food. Big Truck Night runs Friday, July 31, from 5 to 7 p.m. Parking costs $10 Friday through Sunday.

The **Prince William County Fair** runs July 31 through August 8 at the fairgrounds in Manassas, promising rides, sights, and classic Americana entertainment.

The **Clarke County Fair** celebrates its 71st year August 9-15 in Berryville, featuring animal shows and barbecue chicken.

The **Arlington County Fair**, running August 12-16 at Thomas Jefferson Community Center, draws over 150,000 visitors annually. Admission is free, though some activities carry fees. The nearly 50-year-old event ranks among the largest free fairs on the East Coast.

The **Montgomery County Agricultural Fair** takes over 62 acres in Gaithersburg August 14-22, with more than 5,000 animals filling over 50 buildings.

The **Page Valley Fair** runs August 23-29 in Luray against the backdrop of the Blue Ridge Mountains, while the **Shenandoah County Fair** closes out the season August 28 through September 5 in Woodstock.

## Food and Drink Festivals

**Nomini Bay Oyster Festival** arrives at Dirt Farm Brewing in Bluemont August 1-2, serving Virginia-grown oysters raw and grilled.

**BBQ & Bluegrass** pairs American cooking with live music at Breaux Vineyards in Purcellville on August 8.

The **Virginia Bourbon and Beer Festival** hits Fredericksburg Fairgrounds on August 8 from 4 to 9 p.m.

**TASTE Leesburg** fills downtown with local restaurants, food trucks, wine, and beer on August 8 from 5 to 10 p.m.

The **Around The World Cultural Food Festival** brings international cuisine, folk performances, and ethnic artisans to Oronoco Bay Park in Alexandria on August 29.

**The Watermelon Bash** at Great Country Farms in Bluemont runs August 29-30, featuring watermelon-themed games, farm attractions, and a new watermelon carving competition.

## Unique Events Round Out the Month

The **Lake Anne Cardboard Boat Regatta** on August 8 in Reston draws thousands of spectators to watch teams paddle homemade cardboard vessels around the plaza.

**One Loudoun Carnival at Uptown** brings rides, games, and carnival food to Ashburn August 12-23.

The **Leesburg Festival of Kites & Crafts** fills Ida Lee Park with flying kites and artisan vendors on August 22.

**KidsFest** closes out August 29 at Reston Town Center with live music, interactive games, and family entertainment.
