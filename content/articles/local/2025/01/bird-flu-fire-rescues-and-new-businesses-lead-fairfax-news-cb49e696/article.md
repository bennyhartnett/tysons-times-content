Virginia has confirmed bird flu at the Metro Richmond Zoo for the first time in the facility’s 30-year history. Two cases were detected following at least three other recent outbreaks in the Washington region that killed an estimated 1 million chickens and put officials on alert.

Firefighters helped several people evacuate and rescued two dogs from a garden-style apartment fire Wednesday in the 7300 block of Route 29 in West Falls Church. No civilians or firefighters were injured.

A new Onelife Fitness center may be coming to the Mosaic District. A zoning verification letter submitted to Fairfax County indicates the company is considering a facility at 2910 District Avenue spanning more than 30,000 square feet and including one or more pools.

The space was previously occupied by Custom Ink, whose lease expires March 31. The parties involved have not commented on the potential fitness center.

Gene Samburg, founder of West Falls Church-based Kastle Systems, has died at 83. Samburg’s company became widely known for the security cards and key fobs used by thousands of office workers across the Washington area and tens of thousands nationwide.

Virginia state Sen. Stella Pekarsky, a former Fairfax County School Board member, has proposed legislation requiring homeschooling parents to document their teaching qualifications and their children’s academic progress. Current state law includes an exemption for parents who withdraw their children from public school for religious reasons.

Club Pilates Hybla Valley plans to open Thursday, Jan. 23, at Mount Vernon Plaza. The studio, located at 7702 B-D Richmond Highway, specializes in Reformer-based Pilates training and has begun accepting bookings through its app.

Providence District Supervisor Dalia Palchik is hosting an inaugural Lunar New Year open house at the Providence Community Center. The family-friendly celebration will include cultural performances, Chinese calligraphy, art, traditional food and fortune cards.

The Lorton Community Action Center is marking its 50th anniversary in 2025 as demand for its services continues to grow amid an uncertain outlook. The nonprofit advanced its founder’s vision of a centralized “supermarket of human services” when it moved into the new Lorton Community Center in 2022.

Thursday’s forecast calls for mostly sunny skies, a high near 32 degrees and calm winds becoming southerly at about 5 mph. The night will be mostly clear with a low around 17, while calm winds are expected to shift northwest at about 5 mph after midnight.
