George Mason University will bring four of its schools together in Arlington on Tuesday, Feb. 4, for an open house highlighting more than 40 graduate programs.

The in-person event runs from 5:30 to 7 p.m. in the multipurpose room at Van Metre Hall, 3351 Fairfax Drive. The building is accessible from the Virginia Square Metro station on the Orange and Silver lines, as well as nearby Metrobus and ART stops.

Prospective students can meet faculty and admissions representatives, visit program tables and receive a brief résumé review. Part-time and full-time study options will be featured, along with online, in-person and hybrid classes.

Participating schools include the Antonin Scalia Law School, Carter School for Peace and Conflict Resolution, Costello College of Business, and Schar School of Policy and Government.

Advance registration is requested for the Arlington Graduate Open House.
