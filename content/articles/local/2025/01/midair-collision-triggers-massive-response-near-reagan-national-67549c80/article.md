A military helicopter and a regional passenger jet carrying about 60 people collided in midair near Reagan National Airport, prompting a massive emergency response in the surrounding waters. The helicopter was on a training flight from Fort Belvoir’s 12th Aviation Battalion, a military spokesperson said.

In Arlington, police identified Xuewei He, 32, of McLean as the woman killed in a four-vehicle crash Monday, Jan. 27, at Wilson Boulevard and North Glebe Road. Charges are pending against a pickup truck driver who was hospitalized with serious injuries.

Virginia Gov. Glenn Youngkin defended President Donald Trump’s shifting attempt to freeze trillions of dollars in federal spending, arguing Wednesday that voters want major changes in Washington.

The prospect of a smaller federal government could have sweeping consequences across the region. George Mason University researcher Terry Clower said the D.C. area remains heavily dependent on federal agencies, contractors and the jobs they support, despite its sizable technology, education, health care and service sectors.

Two dogs that survived a Nov. 24 animal-rescue flight crash in New York’s Catskill Mountains have found new homes. The crash killed pilot Seuk Kim of Springfield, a volunteer who transported animals from disaster zones and crowded shelters to rescue organizations. His friends are working to honor him by helping more pets.

Playful Pack, a dog day care and boarding center, will open Feb. 3 at 6464-C Lincolnia Road in Barcroft Plaza. An open house is scheduled for Saturday, Feb. 1, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.

The Doomsday Clock moved one second closer to midnight this week, reaching 89 seconds—the closest point to catastrophe in its nearly eight-decade history and its first change in two years.

Fairfax County also launched “Sustain Fairfax,” a public-awareness campaign encouraging residents to take steps that support environmental stewardship and sustainable practices.

Thursday’s forecast calls for sunshine, a high near 48 degrees and light winds shifting southwest at 5 to 7 mph. Rain is expected mainly after 1 a.m. Friday, with an overnight low around 40. The chance of precipitation is 80%, with rainfall totals between one-tenth and one-quarter of an inch.
