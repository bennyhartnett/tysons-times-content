Metro recorded some of its heaviest weekday ridership since the COVID-19 pandemic began in 2020 during the first two days of the week. The increase coincided with federal workers returning to offices, warmer weather and strong demand for events such as Washington Capitals games.

U.S. figure skating champions are heading to Capital One Arena in Washington for the Legacy on Ice concert, which will raise money for families of victims of the Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport crash. Reston resident and 2024 world champion Ilia Malinin is among the performers and has a personal connection to the tribute.

Fairfax County will begin a menstrual-product donation drive Saturday, March 1. New, sealed packages of tampons, pads, liners and menstrual cups will be accepted through March 31 at every library branch, the Fairfax County Government Center and other county facilities. Donations will support local shelters and nonprofit organizations.

The University of Virginia is celebrating the grand opening of its Northern Virginia campus in Merrifield on Friday, February 28. The expansion is intended to improve educational access for working professionals across the Washington metropolitan area.

Falls Church officials are monitoring approximately $50 million in previously approved federal grants amid efforts to reduce federal spending. City Manager Wyatt Shields said about half of the pending money is connected to transportation projects, while the rest covers affordable housing, stormwater work and other areas.

Voting in the 2025 Reston Association Board of Directors election opens at 5 p.m. Friday. Two candidates are seeking positions on the association’s nine-member board.

Crimson Coward will hold customer-appreciation events at its West Springfield and Annandale restaurants from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday. The Nashville-style hot chicken chain will offer buy-one-get-one deals on chicken tenders and conduct a random drawing for prizes including tablets and wireless earbuds.

At Martin Luther King Jr. Park in Gum Springs, the Fairfax County Park Authority plans to replace a baby pool that has been in place for more than 50 years with a splash pad. Although the pool had been scheduled for renovation, community feedback prompted officials to pursue the new design.

Friday’s forecast calls for sunshine and a high near 54 degrees. Northwest winds of 10 to 15 mph will shift southwest during the afternoon, with gusts potentially reaching 25 mph. Clouds will increase overnight as the temperature falls to around 44 degrees.
