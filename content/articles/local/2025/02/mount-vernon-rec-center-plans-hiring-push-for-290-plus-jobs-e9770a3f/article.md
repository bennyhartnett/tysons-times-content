The newly renovated Mount Vernon Rec Center is seeking candidates for more than 290 part-time positions before its planned reopening in May 2025.

The Fairfax County Park Authority scheduled six open hiring events across February, March and April:

- Saturday, Feb. 15, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Belle View Elementary
- Saturday, March 15, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Belle View Elementary
- Sunday, March 23, from noon to 3 p.m. at Martha Washington Library
- Wednesday, April 16, from 4 to 6 p.m. at Martha Washington Library
- Saturday, April 19, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Martha Washington Library
- Monday, April 21, from 5 to 7 p.m. at Martha Washington Library

The openings span multiple departments. Applicants can review available positions and apply online through the Mount Vernon Rec Center jobs webpage. Questions may be sent to FCPAJOBS@fairfaxcounty.gov.
