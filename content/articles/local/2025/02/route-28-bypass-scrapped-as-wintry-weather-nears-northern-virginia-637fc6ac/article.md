A long-planned Route 28 bypass is officially dead, while Northern Virginia residents face a Wednesday night forecast of sleet and freezing rain. Here are the latest local developments for Feb. 5, 2025.

Rep. Don Beyer, D-Va., will host a candlelight vigil at 5:30 p.m. Wednesday for the 67 people killed in the American Airlines crash near Reagan National Airport the previous week. The gathering will be held at Rivergate City Park, 2 Montgomery St., along Alexandria’s waterfront.

A preliminary National Transportation Safety Board report on the crash is not expected for several weeks. Arlington’s representatives in Congress have offered few conclusions about possible causes, though local concerns about crowded airspace and the number of flights at National Airport date back years.

The Prince William Board of County Supervisors voted 5-3 Tuesday to cancel a proposed Route 28 extension. The project would have diverted traffic from Centreville Road before reconnecting north of Bull Run Stream in Fairfax County. Approved in 2020, the plan encountered several obstacles, including insufficient support from Fairfax County.

Virginia lawmakers are also considering the state’s first regulations for automatic license plate readers. The House of Delegates passed legislation Tuesday that would restrict how long police may store collected data and allow the Virginia Department of Transportation to issue installation permits. Privacy advocates caution that the measure could broaden surveillance instead of curbing it.

Loudoun County plans to begin a community process this spring to explore ways to reduce aircraft noise around Dulles International Airport. A virtual kickoff meeting is anticipated in April to introduce the project as residents continue raising concerns about flights over their homes.

Fairfax County Public Schools is developing changes intended to strengthen athletic procedures and transparency before the next school year. A working group is preparing the plan with the Virginia High School League, months after Hayfield Secondary School’s football team withdrew from the playoffs.

A U.S. Secret Service task force inspected thousands of payment devices while targeting a card-skimming operation that affected government-assistance recipients. Investigators visited 879 businesses across the D.C. area, examined 6,561 devices and discovered 24 skimmers.

Hunter Mill District Supervisor Walter Alcorn will hold a town hall from 7 to 8:30 p.m. Thursday at the North County Governmental Center, 1801 Cameron Glen Drive in Reston. Residents are invited to ask about the proposed casino and other district issues.

Wednesday’s forecast calls for a 30% chance of daytime precipitation, with snow and sleet becoming more likely after 1 p.m. The chance rises to 100% overnight, when sleet is expected to shift to freezing rain and sleet between 1 and 4 a.m. Temperatures should reach about 37 degrees before falling to around 32.
