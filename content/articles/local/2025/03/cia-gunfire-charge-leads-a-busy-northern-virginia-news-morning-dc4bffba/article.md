A 28-year-old man faces a federal firearms charge after authorities say he repeatedly fired a handgun outside CIA headquarters during an hours-long barricade Wednesday morning. He allegedly fired several rounds at the facility before shooting himself.

Former U.S. Attorney Jessica D. Aber was found dead Saturday, March 22, in Alexandria. Police responding to a report of an unresponsive woman in the 900 block of Beverley Drive at about 9:18 a.m. pronounced the 43-year-old former Eastern District of Virginia prosecutor dead at the scene.

In Fairfax Station, investigators are working to determine what caused a house fire Saturday afternoon in the 7100 block of Laketree Drive. All occupants evacuated safely, and no civilians or firefighters were injured.

The region’s restaurant industry is warning that financial pressures and federal actions could lead to closures as costs rise and customers cut spending. A recent Restaurant Association Metropolitan Washington survey found that 44% of full-service casual restaurants responding said they were somewhat or very likely to close in 2025.

Fairfax County welcomed 75 new citizens during a naturalization ceremony Thursday at the Government Center. County election workers and the League of Women Voters helped all 75 register to vote, along with several citizens attending the ceremony.

Google is expanding again at Reston Station, taking another floor at 1900 Reston Metro Plaza. The company will now occupy more than 168,000 square feet in the office tower.

FASTSIGNS of Alexandria, a longtime family-owned Richmond Highway business, closed at the end of January. Howard Newman acquired the company in 1991, and his son Kirby joined the operation eight years later.

Forever 21 is preparing to close its U.S. stores through liquidation sales while continuing online operations during the wind-down. The retailer has six Northern Virginia locations: Arlington, Fairfax, Leesburg, Springfield, Woodbridge and Dulles Town Center.

Monday’s forecast calls for showers mainly before 8 a.m., followed by clouds giving way to mostly sunny skies and a high near 66. The chance of rain is 60%, with less than one-tenth of an inch expected. Clouds will increase overnight as temperatures fall to around 43.
