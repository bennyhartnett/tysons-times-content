Springfield District Supervisor Pat Herrity is expected to leave the campaign trail for several weeks after undergoing surgery Thursday, March 13, for a hereditary heart condition. The condition was discovered after Herrity experienced medical problems earlier that week.

Herrity, who is seeking to become Virginia’s next lieutenant governor, said he expects to make a full recovery.

President Donald Trump also said Friday that he would again stop plans to build a new FBI headquarters in Maryland, arguing during a Department of Justice speech that the location was too far from the agency’s current headquarters. Maryland leaders vowed to continue advocating for the move to Greenbelt, which was selected over proposed sites in Springfield and Landover.

At Dulles International Airport, a 54-year-old Frederick man was charged with assault and disorderly conduct after allegedly punching a United Airlines gate agent Thursday night. The employee was hospitalized, and United banned the passenger from future flights.

Sportswriter and bestselling author John Feinstein died March 13 at his brother’s home in McLean. Feinstein, 69, wrote more than 40 books, including “A Season on the Brink,” which chronicled Indiana University men’s basketball coach Bob Knight.

A Fairfax County contractor is facing six criminal charges connected with three alleged fraud cases. Authorities accuse the owner of Capital Pro Services of accepting clients’ money without completing work, including two cases in which homeowners paid for construction projects that were never started.

New U.S. Census Bureau figures show the Washington metropolitan region growing faster than the nation overall. The area lost residents during the COVID-19 pandemic, affecting businesses including offices and restaurants, but its population has since rebounded beyond its earlier level.

Vienna police arrested a 33-year-old man after officers responding to a report of an intoxicated person found him lying on the ground. Police said he ran aggressively toward an officer and attempted an assault before biting the officer while being taken into custody. He was charged with assaulting a law enforcement officer and obstructing justice.

Fairfax County transportation officials are planning two speed humps on Radford Avenue in the Mount Zephyr neighborhood. The improvements will cover the roughly one-third-mile residential stretch between Richmond Highway and Jackson Place.

Monday’s forecast calls for partly sunny skies, a high near 56 degrees and northwest winds of 15 to 17 mph, with gusts reaching 28 mph. Conditions should become mostly clear overnight as temperatures fall to about 36 degrees.
