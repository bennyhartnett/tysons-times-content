Fairfax County shoppers are worried that new 25% tariffs on goods from Canada and Mexico could push grocery bills even higher. At Trader Joe’s in Idylwood, one customer expressed particular concern about the effect on lower- and middle-income households.

Elsewhere, Maryland officials say the prevalence of Virginia license plates on Baltimore streets costs the state millions of dollars. A recent Motor Vehicle Administration report sent to Gov. Wes Moore’s office found that Maryland has little power to address the issue.

Annandale welcomed Lidya Market & Café in late February at 4544-A John Marr Drive. The Ethiopian specialty store carries berbere, shiro, lentils, rice flour, coffee, dates, snacks, incense and other imported products.

The St. James sports complex in Springfield will acquire Washington Little Capitals Inc., a youth hockey development organization with four decades of history. The Little Caps must find a new home rink because the Ion International Training Center in Leesburg plans to close its ice rinks in May and convert the facility into an events and conference center.

In Reston, 86-year-old competitive runner Ecris Williams continues to impress neighbors with her commitment to exercise. The longtime resident’s active life has included both auto racing and foot races.

South Lakes High School is also highlighting inclusion through Matt Ravenstahl’s Inclusive Fine Arts class. Neurotypical and neurodivergent students work together on projects ranging from masks to drawings of international flags.

Registration for Fairfax County Neighborhood and Community Services summer camps opens Tuesday, March 11, 2025. The county offers programs for children and teenagers at locations across Fairfax, with families encouraged to register early.

Framebridge in McLean will hold a private shopping event from 5 to 8 p.m. Thursday, March 13, celebrating its recent grand opening. Customers can bring photographs, artwork and memorabilia for framing, with 10% of sales benefiting the McLean Project for the Arts.

Thursday’s forecast calls for partly sunny skies, a high near 49 degrees and strong winds. West winds are expected to reach 18 to 23 mph during the afternoon, with gusts as high as 46 mph. The night should be mostly clear with a low near 33, while northwest winds of 11 to 18 mph could gust up to 34 mph.
