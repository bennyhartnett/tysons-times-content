A sold-out crowd of more than 400 people helped Senior Services of Alexandria raise over $185,000 during its annual gala on March 29.

Held at the Westin Alexandria Old Town Hotel, the event celebrated the organization’s 57 years of service to Alexandria and recognized community members whose work has benefited the city’s older adults.

Executive Director Mary Lee Anderson missed the gala for the first time after breaking her ankle in a fall. She credited the organization’s staff with ensuring the evening proceeded smoothly.

The 2025 Community Service Awards went to Jack Fannon, Jane King and retired Col. Jim Paige. Lucelle O’Flaherty received the Community Spirit Award.

For the first time, the gala’s silent auction was conducted virtually, allowing participants to place bids online or in person. The evening also included a limited-ticket raffle for tickets to see Billy Joel and Sting perform at Nationals Park in September.

Benchmark at Alexandria served as the event’s presenting sponsor.
