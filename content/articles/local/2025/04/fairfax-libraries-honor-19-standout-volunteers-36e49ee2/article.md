Fairfax County Public Library honored 19 volunteers for their longevity and exceptional service during its April 9 board meeting at George Mason Regional Library.

The recognition came during National Volunteer Month as part of the library system’s annual Star Volunteer Awards. Employees nominate volunteers whose contributions have made a notable difference across library branches and operations.

The awards underscore the scale of volunteer support behind the system’s 23 branches, which serve more than 1 million people. Last year, more than 1,200 volunteers contributed over 78,000 hours while also promoting the value of public libraries throughout the community.

Four people received awards for completing 1,000 hours of service:

- Susan O’Boyle, Burke Centre Library
- Tarun Ramesh, City of Fairfax Regional Library, Patrick Henry Library and Technical Operations
- Elizabeth Adams, Richard Byrd Library
- Alison Duncan, Technical Operations

Fifteen volunteers were recognized for exceptional service:

- Brandon Burrell, Burke Centre Library
- Anjali Ahuja, Chantilly Regional Library
- Claudia Johnston, City of Fairfax Regional Library
- Jacob Hallisey, Great Falls Library
- Elaine Michl, Kings Park Library
- Irene Marx, Lorton Library
- Peggy Jackson, Patrick Henry Library
- Anne Sween, Patrick Henry Library
- Samantha Goldberg, Pohick Regional Library
- Michael Carpenter, Reston Regional Library
- Ray Walser, Reston Regional Library
- Marion Glass, Thomas Jefferson Library
- Mathias Tsige, Tysons-Pimmit Regional Library
- Philip Zettle, Tysons-Pimmit Regional Library
- Steve Corley, Technical Operations
