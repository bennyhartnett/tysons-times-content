Criminals routinely seek personal information they can exploit, but a few basic security measures can make that data more difficult to obtain and misuse.

AARP Virginia recommends creating a strong, unique password or passphrase for every account. A password manager can securely generate and store those credentials.

Enabling multi-factor authentication adds another barrier against unauthorized access. Look in account settings for options labeled two-factor authentication, two-step verification or multi-factor authentication, then follow the setup instructions.

Devices also need current operating systems and antivirus software. Updates sometimes fix known security vulnerabilities, so both should be configured to update automatically.

Suspected scams should be reported to local law enforcement. Anyone seeking help from AARP can call 1-877-908-3360 or visit the AARP Fraud Watch Network.
