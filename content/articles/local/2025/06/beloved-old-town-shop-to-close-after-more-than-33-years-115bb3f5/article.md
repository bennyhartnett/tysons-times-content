Imagine Artwear will close its King Street storefront on June 30, ending Carol Supplee’s run of more than 33 years as a small-business owner in Old Town Alexandria.

Supplee plans to shift the shop online as she focuses on her health. Until the storefront closes, customers can receive 50% off all merchandise.

The business began as Fiber Designs in 1988. Supplee purchased it in 1992 and built a following around American-made goods and handcrafted textiles. She said the designers and suppliers she worked with became like family.

Over the years, the store also developed into a community gathering place where customers met, formed friendships and started conversations. A large outdoor mural added by Supplee became a popular selfie stop for Old Town visitors.

Supplee, a former president of the Old Town Business and Professional Association, has invited customers to leave messages in a book at the shop as its final weeks unfold.

“I love capturing the stories from customers,” Supplee said. “This has been a great opportunity.”
