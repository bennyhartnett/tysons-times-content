Fairfax County residents had a one-week window to apply for several affordable rental housing waitlists, including units with up to three bedrooms across the county.

The Fairfax County Redevelopment and Housing Authority opened applications at 8 a.m. Monday, June 2, 2025, for Project-Based Voucher units in privately owned and county-owned properties. The application period was scheduled to close at 11:59 p.m. Sunday, June 8.

Properties accepting applications included:

- Hollybrook III, 3012 Patrick Henry Drive in Falls Church, with one- and two-bedroom units
- Creekside Village, 7979 Janna Lee Avenue in Alexandria, with one-, two- and three-bedroom units
- The Arden, 2317 Huntington Avenue in Alexandria, with one-, two- and three-bedroom units
- One University–The Robinson, 4530 University Drive in Fairfax, with two-bedroom units
- Madison Ridge, 14806 Rydell Road in Centreville, with one- and two-bedroom units
- Ovation at Arrowbrook, 13325 Fairfield Ridge Avenue in Herndon, with one- and two-bedroom units

Applications were also being accepted for one- and three-bedroom Rental Assistance Demonstration Project-Based Voucher units owned and operated by the housing authority. Briarcliff II in Vienna was not included.

Applicants could submit forms through the Fairfax County Housing and Community Development website. Instructions were available in English and Spanish.

People needing assistance could call 703-246-5100 or visit the housing authority’s office at 3700 Pender Drive in Fairfax between 8 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. Monday through Friday.

Applications were to be selected randomly for placement on the waiting lists. When a unit became available, applicants would be contacted and screened for eligibility based on household income, household size and the size of the unit.

Under the Project-Based Voucher program, participating households pay a portion of the rent, up to 35% of adjusted gross household income. The housing authority pays the remainder directly to the property owner. Participants cannot receive other federal housing-assistance subsidies.
