A Fairfax County jury awarded $2.4 million to the parents of 15-year-old Leeyan Hanjia Yan, one of two Oakton High School students killed in a June 2022 crash as they walked away from school. The civil case placed responsibility on the unlicensed 18-year-old driver and his father, who had given him a new BMW.

Tysons continues to face elevated office vacancies. CBRE’s latest quarterly data puts the submarket’s vacancy rate at 26%, down from a recent peak of 27.2% in the third quarter of 2024. Herndon has Northern Virginia’s highest rate at 29.8%.

CBRE’s figure exceeds CoStar’s 19.7% estimate used by the Tysons Community Alliance. The alliance has highlighted recent office-building purchases and increased employee visits as encouraging signs.

Gov. Glenn Youngkin reaffirmed his support for Immigration and Customs Enforcement arrests at courthouses. His position has drawn criticism because some detainees reportedly had no criminal records and were appearing in court to pay traffic-related fines.

Fairfax County is also revisiting plans for the Mount Vernon Governmental Center near Sherwood Hall Lane and Parkers Lane. Officials and members of an architectural and engineering team met with residents June 24 to discuss preliminary work on the Mount Vernon Master Plan, which remains in its early visioning and planning stage.

In Chantilly, Stratos Solutions Inc. plans to invest $1.58 million to expand its headquarters into a newly acquired, 5,000-square-foot space at 14840 Conference Center Drive. The employee-owned technical services company, which supports the U.S. intelligence community, expects the expansion to create 28 jobs.

Residents of Bailey’s Crossroads and Seven Corners are weighing how their communities should appear on new gateway signs. Ashton Design representatives presented public survey findings at a Crossroads + Corners Coalition meeting before gathering feedback on proposed concepts.

A new Virginia historical marker was unveiled June 29 at the Little Hunting Park Pool and Tennis Club in Bucknell Manor. It commemorates the 1969 U.S. Supreme Court case Sullivan v. Little Hunting Park, which helped pave the way for ending segregation at community recreational clubs nationwide.

Showers and thunderstorms are expected throughout Monday, with some storms capable of producing heavy rain. The forecast calls for partly sunny skies, a high near 89 and a 70% chance of precipitation. Storm chances fall to 30% Monday night, mainly before 7 p.m., followed by partly cloudy conditions and a low around 75.
