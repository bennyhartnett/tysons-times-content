Capital Bikeshare will raise prices across every membership tier beginning Aug. 1, its first price adjustment since 2021. The bike-sharing system cited record ridership growth and increasing operating expenses.

Fairfax County police also announced that an undercover investigation by the department’s Child Exploitation Unit resulted in the arrests of 16 men accused of sexually soliciting a minor. The suspects, who range in age from 20 to 65, face more than 80 felony charges combined.

A Salvadoran father has spoken publicly after being released from nearly two months in Immigration and Customs Enforcement detention. Alexandria police arrested the man before transferring him to ICE. His attorney said a judge had issued a warrant after he missed a Fairfax County court hearing involving a domestic case that has since closed.

In Herndon, a family filed a complaint with the Virginia attorney general’s office alleging that three children younger than 11 were expelled from the Nysmith School for the Gifted after experiencing antisemitic bullying.

New York-based luxury real estate brokerage Serhant has entered the Washington market by adding a team of prominent McLean agents. The firm was founded by Ryan Serhant, star of Netflix’s “Owning Manhattan” and a former nine-season cast member of Bravo’s “Million Dollar Listing New York.” Its luxury listings from New York to Florida reach as high as $80 million.

Northern Virginia newspapers are experimenting with new models as digital news increasingly dominates the industry. Rappahannock Media, which publishes Inside NoVA, is working to balance print and online coverage, while Loudoun Now recently converted into a nonprofit.

In Reston, firefighters from Fire Station 25’s A-shift stopped to help a turtle cross Sunset Hills Road near Reston Parkway. Firefighter Ben Welty posed with the animal before the crew and turtle continued on their separate ways.

Loudoun County is considering whether a tiny-home community could expand affordable housing options. Board of Supervisors Chair Phyllis Randall expressed enthusiasm during a Monday business meeting about the possibility of helping residents with low and moderate incomes purchase homes.

Thursday’s forecast calls for sunshine and a high near 89 degrees, followed by mostly clear skies and a low around 68. Independence Day is expected to remain sunny, with a high near 87 and a north wind around 6 mph.
