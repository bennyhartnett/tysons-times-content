Families affected by the deadly midair collision between a passenger jet and a military helicopter near Reagan National Airport are pressing the Army for greater transparency and action. Dozens of relatives said in a letter to the Army’s top official that the service has avoided accountability and oversight in the months since the crash.

Elsewhere across Northern Virginia on Thursday, July 10, officials are warning about traffic, thunderstorms and potentially harmful algae.

A WNBA game expected to attract about 5,000 people to EagleBank Arena will increase traffic around George Mason University’s campus from 4 to 10 p.m. Drivers and visitors can subscribe to Mason Alert for information about heavily affected areas and alternate routes.

Reston Association watershed staff have also found a small occurrence of potentially harmful blue-green algae in Lake Anne. Swimming is never allowed in the lake, and residents are being advised to keep dogs away from the water. Blue-green algae was observed in Lake Audubon earlier in July.

Herndon Reston Indivisible will hold a “Good Trouble Lives On” rally honoring civil rights leader and U.S. Rep. John Lewis from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. Thursday, July 17, at Baron Cameron Park, 11300 Baron Cameron Ave. in Reston. The organization’s June 14 “No Kings” rallies, part of a nationwide anti-Trump protest movement, drew an estimated 2,000 people.

Falls Church officials are considering allowing public safety aides to issue parking tickets, direct traffic and respond to abandoned vehicles. Current city code reserves parking and traffic-control duties for sworn law enforcement officers.

Visitors will have to wait until December to see George Washington’s fully renovated mansion at Mount Vernon. Parts of the building have been closed since November during a comprehensive restoration project that began in 2019. The mansion, except for its cellar, is scheduled to remain open during the final construction phase from December through September 2026.

Fairfax County Public Schools Adult and Community Education is accepting scholarship applications from current and prospective students pursuing skilled-trades careers. The $1,000 awards can be applied to apprenticeship classes beginning in the fall. Applications are due Aug. 15.

Big Buns Damn Good Burgers will mark National French Fry Day on Friday, July 11, by offering free regular or Cajun fries with the purchase of a burger, sandwich or bowl. Customers must use the code `FRYORCRY25` online or mention the promotion in a restaurant.

Showers and thunderstorms are expected throughout Thursday, with mostly cloudy skies, a high near 85 degrees and a 60% chance of precipitation. The chance of rain and storms drops to 30% overnight, when temperatures are expected to fall to around 72.
