Fairfax County Fire and Rescue Department recognized its top uniformed, civilian and volunteer members during its annual Of the Year Awards on Friday, Nov. 7, 2025. The honors are the department’s highest commendations.

Captain Christopher Seaman was named Career Officer of the Year. HazMat Technician MaryKate Black received Career Firefighter of the Year, Kyle Cash was named Civilian Employee of the Year, and Paramedic John Gutsmiedl earned Operational Volunteer of the Year.
