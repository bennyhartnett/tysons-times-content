After four-year breaks, Arctic Monkeys and Harry Styles returned Thursday, Jan. 22, with new singles that quickly climbed the charts and energized indie and pop-rock fans.

## Arctic Monkeys Support War Child With “Opening Night”

Arctic Monkeys partnered with War Child for “Opening Night,” a track featured on the organization’s newly announced charity album, *HELP(2)*.

The collaborative record brings together prominent artists to spotlight the experiences of children in war-affected regions. Proceeds from the album, scheduled for release March 6, will support War Child’s work protecting children living through conflict.

“Opening Night” builds on Arctic Monkeys’ established alternative sound. A steady rhythm anchors the track as layers of instrumentation gradually gather around the lead vocals. Its elusive lyrics and lonely atmosphere make the song feel familiar to longtime listeners, although fans hoping for a full new album may find the standalone release leaves them wanting more.

## Harry Styles Turns Up the Synths on “Aperture”

Styles takes a brighter, electronic direction with “Aperture,” the first taste of his upcoming album, *Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally*.

Driven by synthesizers and an upbeat dance rhythm, the pop track pairs a lively backdrop with Styles’ calm vocal delivery. Its lyrics explore the fear involved in falling in love and the eventual acceptance of that vulnerability.

The combination creates a comforting, danceable techno-pop anthem that previews the mood of Styles’ new album, also due March 6.

Together, the two comeback singles mark major returns for both artists while giving listeners plenty to anticipate.
