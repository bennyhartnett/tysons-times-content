Fairfax County is set to receive a $4.56 million grant aimed at improving roadway safety.

The award would provide the county with funding for road safety improvements, although the archived material does not identify the specific projects, locations, construction schedule or conditions attached to the grant. It also does not name the agency providing the money or indicate when the funds will become available.

The county’s original online announcement is no longer accessible at its listed location. Instead, the page displays a notice stating that the requested page could not be found.

Residents seeking more information about the grant or the projects it may support can contact Fairfax County at 703-FAIRFAX, or 703-324-7329. The county lists its government center at 12000 Government Center Parkway in Fairfax.
