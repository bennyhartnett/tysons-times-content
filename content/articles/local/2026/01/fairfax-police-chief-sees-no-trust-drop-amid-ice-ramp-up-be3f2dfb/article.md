Fairfax County Police Chief Kevin Davis says federal immigration enforcement efforts under President Donald Trump’s administration have not produced evidence of declining community trust in local police.

Davis pointed to domestic violence calls as one measure of whether residents feel comfortable contacting officers. Fairfax County recorded no substantial change in that data during 2025, he said.

Drivers could face delays on northbound Route 29 as widening work continues. Weather permitting, the right lane between Clifton Road/Stringfellow Road and Sandy Point Lane will close from 9:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Jan. 7-9 and Jan. 12-16 for paving-related work. Motorists are advised to consider alternate routes.

Abigail Spanberger is preparing to become Virginia’s 75th governor and the first woman to lead the commonwealth. The Democrat is scheduled to take the oath of office outside the Virginia State Capitol on Jan. 17 before beginning a four-year term.

A 63-year-old Fairfax man faces nine charges, including one felony, after allegedly trying to flee a traffic stop early Sunday in Fairfax City. Officers reportedly saw a vehicle speeding and swerving near Fairfax Boulevard and Fair Woods Parkway shortly after 2 a.m.

Tysons-based government contractor 22nd Century Technologies, also known as TSCTI, acquired BT Federal Inc. on Dec. 30. The Reston company was a subsidiary of London-listed telecommunications business BT Group. No purchase price was disclosed, but CEO Anil Sharma described it as TSCTI’s largest acquisition yet and its fourth in slightly more than two years.

Vienna resident Audrey McKeen has signed a professional contract with the National Women’s Soccer League’s Racing Louisville. The 17-year-old Virginia Development Academy player will officially join the club in 2026 under a deal running through the 2028 season, making her one of the league’s youngest players.

Barboursville Vineyards’ Octagon 2017 has been named one of the Top 50 U.S. Wines of the Year by British wine publication Decanter. The Virginia winery’s signature red blend earned its place after more than 3,500 American wines were reviewed.

Falls Church Mayor Letty Hardi secured another two-year term in the post after a narrow City Council vote Monday. Her colleagues selected her by a 4-3 margin, with newly elected Council member Arthur Agin casting the deciding vote.

Two new legal notices involve alcohol licenses. One restaurant is seeking permission for on- and off-premises wine and beer sales, while another applicant is seeking an off-premises wine and beer license. Objections to either application are due within 30 days.

Wednesday’s forecast calls for mostly sunny skies and a high near 57 degrees. Northwest winds of 6 to 11 mph could gust as high as 23 mph. The night should be mostly clear with a low near 36, while winds ease to about 6 mph and become calm by evening.
