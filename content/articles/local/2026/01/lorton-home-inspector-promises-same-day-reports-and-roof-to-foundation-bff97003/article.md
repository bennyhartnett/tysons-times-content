Square One Home Inspections offers residential property inspections across Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C., and surrounding communities.

The Lorton-based company inspects single-family houses, condominiums, townhouses, multifamily properties and newly built homes. It also provides pre-listing and maintenance inspections.

Founder and lead inspector James Fletcher holds Virginia license No. 3380000966 and is certified by the International Association of Certified Home Inspectors. According to the company, Fletcher has more than 15 years of experience in residential construction and inspection.

Inspections can cover roofs, foundations, framing, basements, crawlspaces, attics, siding, drainage and other exterior and structural components. The company also evaluates electrical, plumbing and HVAC systems, water heaters, appliances, insulation and interior features such as walls, ceilings, doors, windows, cabinets and bathrooms.

Square One says it may use drones to examine tall roofs and other areas that are difficult to reach. Clients typically receive a digital report with photographs on the same day as the inspection.

The company also offers follow-up explanations, home-maintenance guidance and contractor recommendations. Its inspections are noninvasive, however, meaning concealed problems such as hidden mold, termite damage or structural defects may require evaluation by a specialist.

Square One advertises more than 500 five-star reviews and a $25,000 InterNACHI-backed honor guarantee. Buyers facing short real-estate deadlines may need to schedule early, particularly when seeking a pre-offer inspection.
