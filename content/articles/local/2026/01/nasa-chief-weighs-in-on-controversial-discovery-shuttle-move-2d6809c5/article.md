NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman has addressed the proposed relocation of the Space Shuttle Discovery from Virginia to Texas, suggesting another spacecraft could be sent to Houston if moving Discovery proves impossible.

Isaacman, who recently became NASA administrator under President Donald Trump, told CNBC that the agency could consider an alternative spacecraft for the Johnson Space Center. The proposal to remove Discovery from its Virginia home has generated controversy.

Elsewhere in Northern Virginia, business owners at Barcroft Plaza are determining how to recover after a fire swept through the shopping center Thursday morning. The damaged businesses included a martial arts studio, swimming school and Benjamin Moore paint store.

Fairfax County Police Chief Kevin Davis reported declines in several crime categories during 2025. Homicides fell 15% from 2024, he said, crediting emerging technology, recruitment and retention initiatives, and an increased emphasis on road safety.

Virginia’s transition to clean energy is facing obstacles five years after passage of the Virginia Clean Economy Act. Rapid growth in data centers and artificial intelligence has increased electricity demand, prompting utilities to turn to dirtier fuels under exemptions included in the law.

A Virginia woman’s collection of more than 1,100 paper napkins has found a permanent home at the Library of Virginia. LaRoy accumulated the items over decades, storing them in a blanket box beneath her bed before donating them to the library.

State legislators representing McLean and Great Falls are holding two town halls ahead of the General Assembly session. Rip Sullivan and Sen. Jennifer B. Boysko will appear at Great Falls Library at 6:30 p.m. Monday and at the McLean Community Center at 6:30 p.m. Tuesday.

The City of Fairfax is also offering free public-safety training at Fire Station 3 from February through May. Classes will cover hands-only CPR and AED use, bleeding control, stroke awareness, fire extinguishers and smoke alarms.

Monday’s forecast calls for partly sunny skies, a high near 45 degrees and southerly winds of 5 to 10 mph, with gusts reaching 18 mph. Skies will become mostly cloudy overnight as the temperature falls to around 35.
