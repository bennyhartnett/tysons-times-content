Released Jan. 9, 2026, *People We Meet on Vacation* quickly climbed to No. 2 on Netflix’s Top 10 Movies list, attracting 23.3 million views during its first week.

The standalone adaptation of Emily Henry’s contemporary romance novel follows Poppy and Alex, two friends whose sharply different personalities bring energy and depth to their evolving relationship.

Poppy, played by Emily Bader, is an adventurous and impulsive travel-company employee who explores the world for a living. Traveling gives her a sense of freedom while offering an escape from painful experiences in her hometown.

Alex, portrayed by Tom Blyth, is her opposite: calm, methodical and committed to routines that include morning runs and carefully planned itineraries. As the pair learn more about each other’s lives, their contrasting approaches draw them closer.

The story moves between past and present, gradually revealing new details about their relationship. Colorful, lively scenes complement Poppy’s bright personality and keep the romance visually engaging.

Bader captures Poppy’s enthusiasm, spontaneity and distinctive style, while Blyth convincingly embodies Alex’s composed nature. Their natural chemistry holds up as the characters’ relationship changes throughout the film.

With engaging performances, a layered timeline and carefully chosen details, *People We Meet on Vacation* delivers a lively romance centered on the enduring pull between two opposites.
