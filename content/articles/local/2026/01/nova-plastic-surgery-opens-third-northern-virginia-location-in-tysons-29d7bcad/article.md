NOVA Plastic Surgery & Dermatology has opened a new office at The Boro in Tysons, expanding the Ashburn-based aesthetic practice to its third Northern Virginia location.

Scheduled appointments began Jan. 21. During the soft-opening period, the practice will also accept walk-ins for consultations and injectable services from 3 to 6 p.m. Jan. 27 and from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Jan. 29.

Expanded hours and the full range of services are expected to begin in February.

The new facility offers plastic surgery, cosmetic dermatology, hair transplantation, advanced skincare and nonsurgical aesthetic treatments. The practice is led by board-certified plastic surgeon Dr. Fadi Nukta.

NOVA’s other locations include its flagship Ashburn office and SkinLab by NOVA at One Loudoun. It also operates a Quad A-certified surgery center.

The practice has appeared on the Inc. 5000 list of fastest-growing private companies and has received multiple Best of Loudoun awards.

The Boro is a mixed-use development near the Greensboro and Spring Hill Metro stations, with residential, office, retail, dining and entertainment space. Charlie Schwieger, vice president of asset management at The Meridian Group, said NOVA adds to the development’s selection of wellness and neighborhood services.
