Central Title & Escrow has served residential and commercial real estate clients from Annandale since 2003, offering title and settlement services across the Washington-area suburbs.

The Virginia company handles title searches, title insurance, escrow services and closing logistics. It also accommodates mobile and virtual signings, short-sale and foreclosure transactions, and bilingual closings for Spanish-speaking clients.

President Jennifer Ploutis leads a small team of escrow officers, settlement agents and business-development staff. Central Title promotes a single-contact approach in which one coordinator oversees title requirements, lender communications and the final settlement.

The firm says it provides flexible scheduling and can complete many deliverables within 24 to 48 hours. It also offers educational workshops and co-marketing opportunities for real estate agents and lenders.

As an independent regional agency, Central Title may offer different pricing, title-insurance products and underwriting partners than national companies. Buyers, agents and lenders should confirm those details early, particularly for complex transactions. Advance reservations may also help secure weekend and end-of-month closings.

Central Title & Escrow is located at 7617 Little River Turnpike, Suite 120, in Annandale. Its services are aimed at clients in Annandale, Falls Church and surrounding Fairfax County communities who prefer a small-team approach to the closing process.
