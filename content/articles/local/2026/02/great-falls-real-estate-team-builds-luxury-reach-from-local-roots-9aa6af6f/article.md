Dianne Van Volkenburg has spent nearly three decades working in Great Falls real estate, developing a neighborhood-focused practice into the Great Falls Great Homes team.

Affiliated with Long & Foster, the small team specializes in high-value homes, acreage and land in Great Falls and elsewhere in Northern Virginia. Its services span sales, listing coordination, marketing and client care.

The group markets estate properties through professional staging, video tours, twilight photography and targeted digital campaigns. Luxury real estate channels also help expose listings to prospective buyers beyond the region.

Van Volkenburg’s experience includes multi-acre parcels, custom-home sites and move-in-ready estates. Among the properties promoted by the team are a five-acre Tally Ho parcel for private buyers or custom builders and luxury homes generally priced from $1 million to $5 million.

The team has appeared on regional production rankings and in Long & Foster’s Elite and Platinum producer groups. Public profiles have also reported more than $1 billion in Great Falls closings during Van Volkenburg’s career.

Clients posting public reviews frequently cite the team’s responsiveness, local knowledge, negotiation skills and ability to manage complicated transactions. Its lean structure is designed to provide close assistance throughout the buying or selling process.

Van Volkenburg and her colleagues also sponsor local events and maintain ties within the Great Falls community. The team operates from an office at 758 Walker Road, Suite A, in Great Falls.
