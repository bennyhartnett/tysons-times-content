Sunrise of Vienna brings assisted living and memory care to a walkable stretch of Maple Avenue, where an on-site café open to the public helps connect residents with the surrounding community.

The senior living community at 374 Maple Avenue West offers studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom residences equipped with safety features and emergency response systems. Its common areas include large windows, contemporary furnishings and lounges designed for socializing.

The property is part of Sunrise Senior Living, a company founded by Paul and Terry Klaassen in 1981. Headquartered in McLean, the provider operates more than 270 communities across the United States and Canada.

One distinctive feature of the Vienna location is the Tango Pastry café, which welcomes residents, visitors and members of the public. The arrangement gives families and neighbors another way to spend time with residents while keeping the community connected to life in town.

Sunrise of Vienna provides assisted-living support for residents who need help with bathing, dressing, medication management or mobility. Individual care plans are updated as residents’ needs change, with an emphasis on preserving independence.

Its memory-care program serves people living with Alzheimer’s disease and other forms of dementia. Specially trained staff use structured routines, purposeful activities and sensory engagement in a secure setting intended to reduce anxiety and encourage connection.

Daily life also includes restaurant-style dining, fitness classes, art workshops, music, discussion groups, seasonal events and social gatherings. Local outings and partnerships offer additional opportunities for residents to remain involved in Vienna and the broader Fairfax County community.

Resident and family reviews cited in the source material commonly describe attentive employees, modern surroundings, social activities and a welcoming atmosphere. Some reviews also raise concerns about staff responsiveness, underscoring the importance of touring the property, meeting caregivers and asking detailed questions before choosing a community.
