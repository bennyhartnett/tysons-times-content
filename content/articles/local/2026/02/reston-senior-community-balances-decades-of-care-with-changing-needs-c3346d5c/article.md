Tall Oaks Assisted Living has served older adults and their families in Fairfax County since 1988, making the North Shore Drive community one of Reston’s longest-established senior living options.

The wooded campus offers assisted living, memory care and short-term respite stays in a residential setting. Although development around the Silver Line has transformed parts of Reston, Tall Oaks has retained its quieter, more traditional surroundings.

Located about a mile from the Wiehle–Reston East Metro station, the community combines access to major roads, parks, shops, restaurants and medical offices with a setting removed from much of the area’s traffic and noise.

Residents can choose between studio and one-bedroom apartments designed for accessibility. Typical features include climate control, closet space, private or shared bathroom options and utilities bundled into the monthly rate. Many apartments overlook the surrounding trees.

## Care That Can Change With Residents

Tall Oaks provides several levels of support under one roof, allowing care to be adjusted as residents’ health and daily needs change.

Assisted-living residents can receive individualized help with bathing, dressing, medication management and mobility. Licensed nursing staff are available around the clock.

The community also operates secure memory-care areas for people living with Alzheimer’s disease and other forms of dementia. Its approach includes structured schedules, cognitive activities, closer supervision and routines intended to reduce confusion and encourage engagement.

Respite care is available for short-term residents, including people recovering after a hospital discharge and those whose family caregivers need temporary assistance. Furnished apartments are offered for stays of up to 60 days.

Tall Oaks uses month-to-month leases rather than requiring the large upfront payments found at some retirement communities, giving families more flexibility during periods of transition.

## Meals, Activities and Social Life

Restaurant-style dining provides freshly prepared meals and can accommodate dietary restrictions. Shared dining areas also give residents a regular place to socialize.

Scheduled activities include exercise classes, games, holiday celebrations, seasonal events and group outings. Residents can participate as much or as little as they choose, whether that means attending daily programs or spending a quieter afternoon in a lounge or outdoor area.

Online reviews describe a range of family experiences. Positive comments frequently mention compassionate, long-serving employees, approachable caregivers, engaging memory-care programs, dining and the community’s home-like atmosphere.

Other reviewers have raised concerns about staffing consistency, evening and weekend response times, and communication with administrators. Families considering any senior community may benefit from touring more than once, visiting at different times and speaking with residents’ relatives when possible.

Important questions include how staffing changes between daytime and overnight shifts, how emergencies are handled, how families learn about changes in a resident’s health and what dementia-care training employees receive.

Tall Oaks offers continuity in a part of Reston increasingly shaped by newer development. Its longevity may reassure some families, while others may prioritize more modern surroundings. The final decision depends on each resident’s care needs, preferences and comfort with the community.
