Sushi Sato, a Japanese restaurant that opened in Washington, D.C., late last year, pairs modern American touches with striking interior design and a fine-dining atmosphere.

The restaurant’s most popular menu item, the Dragon Roll, earns a 5-out-of-5 rating. Shrimp tempura and cucumber provide a satisfying crunch, while ripe, creamy avocado covers the roll. Sweet eel sauce balances the spicy mayonnaise, and fresh wasabi adds a final kick.

The Double Shrimp Tempura Roll receives 3 out of 5. Its double-fried preparation delivers extra crunch, but the texture feels slightly overdone. Properly cooked rice softens that intensity and creates a better balance. The cream cheese filling, however, introduces an unexpected flavor that may not appeal to diners seeking a more traditional Japanese experience.

The Salmon Sashimi scores 4 out of 5 for its freshness and delicate, smooth texture. Soy sauce adds a restrained salty note without overwhelming the salmon’s natural flavor, and each soft slice nearly melts in the mouth. Careful plating also makes the dish visually impressive.

Overall, Sushi Sato offers fresh, well-prepared food in a welcoming setting. The Dragon Roll is the strongest recommendation, while the Salmon Sashimi provides a worthwhile alternative. With both classic Japanese dishes and modern flavors, the restaurant has options for sushi newcomers and devoted fans alike.
