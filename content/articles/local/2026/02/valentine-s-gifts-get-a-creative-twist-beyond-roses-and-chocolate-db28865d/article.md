Valentine’s Day staples such as roses, teddy bears and chocolate remain reliable gifts, but more unusual alternatives can add a personal touch to the celebration.

Red roses are a classic, widely available choice for nearly any partner. For something longer-lasting, preserved “eternity flowers” retain their vibrant colors for years and can serve as an enduring reminder of the relationship.

A traditional teddy bear is another convenient, budget-friendly option, with stores ranging from Build-A-Bear to CVS offering different sizes and prices. A Warmie provides a distinctive alternative: These weighted stuffed animals can be heated in a microwave, come in numerous designs and double as comforting stress relievers.

Chocolate lovers may appreciate a familiar Hershey’s bar, a longtime household favorite. Couples seeking a more playful candy experience can try BeanBoozled, a game in which similar-looking jelly beans may have either pleasant or unpleasant flavors.

Shared activities offer another path for partners who value quality time. A couples pottery class at TealNest combines time together with a handmade keepsake from the occasion.

Sur La Table’s couples cooking classes encourage teamwork and end with something participants can eat. A couples perfume-making experience at Olfactory provides a day together and the opportunity to create matching fragrances.
