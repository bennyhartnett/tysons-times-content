For shoppers struggling to find the right bra, Trousseau offers an unusually deep selection on Maple Avenue in Vienna. The independently owned boutique says it keeps more than 4,500 bras in over 160 sizes available while providing personalized fittings and alterations.

Located at 306 Maple Avenue West, Trousseau has served the Washington metropolitan area since opening in 1999. Business records list Michael Wiener as the owner.

Staff members trained in bra sizing help customers identify suitable sizes and styles. The broad inventory includes options for shoppers whose sizes can be difficult to find at conventional retailers.

Beyond everyday bras and lingerie, the store carries lace designs, coordinated sets, sleepwear, loungewear and foundation garments. Its special-occasion selection includes bustiers, shapewear and undergarments intended to fit beneath wedding gowns, along with custom veils and other bridal accessories.

Gift cards, bath products and intimate-apparel gifts are also available for holidays, anniversaries and birthdays.

Customer ratings across review platforms generally range from about 4.1 to 4.6 stars out of five, based on hundreds of reviews. Many customers praise the knowledgeable fitting staff, extensive size selection and welcoming treatment of different body types and fit needs.

Some reviewers, however, cite prices above those at big-box stores. A smaller number report feeling rushed or pressured, while others raise concerns about return and alteration policies. Experiences appear to vary, making it worthwhile for shoppers to understand store policies before purchasing.

Trousseau is best suited to customers who value specialized fitting assistance and selection over bargain pricing or the convenience of a quick retail stop. After more than two decades in Vienna, the boutique remains a local destination for everyday foundations, hard-to-find sizes and bridal undergarments.
