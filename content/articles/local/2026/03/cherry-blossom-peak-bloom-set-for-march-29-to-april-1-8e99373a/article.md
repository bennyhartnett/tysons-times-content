Washington’s Tidal Basin cherry blossoms are expected to reach peak bloom between March 29 and April 1, the National Park Service announced Thursday. The National Cherry Blossom Festival will run from March 20 through April 12, bringing events across the D.C. region.

Fairfax County school leaders are considering tougher discipline as some students use artificial intelligence to create fake nude images of classmates. Under a proposed policy, students responsible for such deepfakes could receive suspensions of up to five days and referrals to the superintendent and law enforcement.

Safety improvements remain pending on Lee Chapel Road more than three years after a crash killed two teenagers. Work to remove two hills between Fairfax County Parkway and Ox Road is expected to begin in 2027. Officials are also coordinating with the Virginia Department of Transportation on a roadside memorial.

Virginia lawmakers are moving toward putting into state law the protections established by a 1982 U.S. Supreme Court ruling that guarantees children access to public education regardless of immigration or citizenship status. The legislation comes amid increased immigration enforcement, federal policy shifts and related court challenges.

A proposal allowing multifamily housing by right in commercial zones failed in the Virginia Senate on Wednesday, March 4. Senators rejected HB 816, introduced by Del. Dan Helmer, in a 17-22 vote despite restrictions proposed by Sen. Barbara Favola. The House of Delegates sent a companion measure to committee.

Virginia restaurants could soon face a lower food-sales requirement for liquor licenses. Legislation passed by both General Assembly chambers would reduce the minimum ratio of food to alcohol sales from 45% to 30%. Gov. Abigail Spanberger could sign the measure, which would represent one of the state’s most significant liquor-license changes in more than 30 years.

A mural is beginning to take shape on the long-vacant building at 7219 Columbia Pike in Annandale. The property is being covered with designs ahead of painting as Annandale Social LLC renovates it for a restaurant and carryout business. An application to sell beer, wine and mixed drinks remains pending.

Reston Community Center is seeking performers, artisans, culturally inspired businesses and community organizations for the 25th anniversary of its Multicultural Festival. The annual fall celebration typically attracts thousands of visitors.

Two new legal notices have also been posted. One concerns an application for a mixed-beverage restaurant license, with objections due within 30 days. The other announces a March 19 public hearing on an advanced academics center, scheduled for 6:45 p.m.

Friday’s forecast calls for morning fog to clear before 11 a.m., followed by mostly cloudy skies and a high near 54. Showers are possible after 4 p.m., with a 20% chance of rain and east winds of 5 to 8 mph. Showers could return after 7 p.m., with an overnight low around 45 and a 30% chance of precipitation.
