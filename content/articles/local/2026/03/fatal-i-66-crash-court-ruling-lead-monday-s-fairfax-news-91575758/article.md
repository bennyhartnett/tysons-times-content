A driver died Saturday afternoon after a Toyota sedan struck the rear of a legally parked tractor-trailer at the eastbound Interstate 66 rest area near Manassas. Virginia State Police said the car caught fire following the collision.

A federal judge on Friday blocked Virginia from enforcing a social media law that required age verification and limited users younger than 16 to one hour per day. The measure, signed by former Gov. Glenn Youngkin, took effect Jan. 1 and was challenged by technology trade group NetChoice.

In Annandale, a woman suffered life-threatening injuries after a vehicle struck her near John Marr Drive and Ravensworth Road on Saturday, Feb. 28. John Marr Drive closed during the investigation and reopened shortly before 9 p.m.

A retired electrician won $1 million with a Powerball ticket purchased Dec. 13 at Express 1 Food Mart, 4713 Backlick Road in Annandale. The Virginia Lottery identified the winner Feb. 24, reporting that he took a month to realize the ticket was a winner.

Reston native Becky Lane made a record-setting powerlifting debut at age 75 on Feb. 14, breaking the national record three times. She is scheduled to compete on the national stage in Anaheim, California, near the end of May.

Fortunoff Backyard Store plans to open an 11,000-square-foot Springfield location in April. The outdoor furniture and patio accessories retailer leased space at Springfield Commons, 6721B Frontier Drive.

Herndon-Reston Indivisible will hold an International Women’s Day protest from 2 to 3:30 p.m. Sunday, March 8, at Reston Parkway and Baron Cameron Avenue. Organizers say the demonstration will support women broadly, with particular attention to women they describe as victims of Jeffrey Epstein and Donald Trump.

Some of the world’s top lacrosse players have gathered at the St. James in Springfield for the Lexus Championship Series, which began Friday night. Rachael DeCecco, senior vice president of lacrosse for the men’s and women’s leagues, said the tournament will offer a preview of the sport’s appearance at the 2028 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles.

Monday is expected to be cloudy, with a high near 36 degrees and a 50% chance of precipitation. Snow could begin after 1 p.m., then change to rain or freezing rain between 1 and 4 a.m. as temperatures fall to around 30. The overnight precipitation chance is 80%, though little snow or ice accumulation is expected.
