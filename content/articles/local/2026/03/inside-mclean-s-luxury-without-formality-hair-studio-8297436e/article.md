Colour Bar Studio has served clients in McLean since 2012, offering customized hair services from its location in the Tysons Boro district.

Founder Tania Ferrel is an American Board-Certified Hair Colorist, Great Lengths Hair Extension specialist and former Wella educator. Her specialties include balayage, dimensional and corrective color, hair extensions and vibrant red shades. She also travels internationally to continue learning new techniques.

The salon’s services include precision cuts, highlights, perms, blowouts and conditioning treatments. Stylists begin with detailed consultations intended to match each service to the client’s hair texture, history, routine and goals. The team also participates in regular training.

Colour Bar Studio describes its approach as “Luxury without formality,” combining an upscale setting with a relaxed atmosphere. The business has earned ratings ranging from 4.5 to 4.8 stars across Google and other review platforms, based on more than 100 reviews.

The salon is located at 1640 Boro Place, Suite 204, in McLean. It is closed Mondays and open the remainder of the week.
