A recreational advisory for the Potomac River has been lifted after water quality improved following a major sewage spill. A section of the Potomac Interceptor collapsed Jan. 19, releasing hundreds of millions of gallons of untreated sewage into the river.

Iranian Americans across the D.C. region are divided over U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran during the weekend. While community members share hopes for safety and a better future for Iranians, they disagree about whether the attacks will help achieve those goals.

The Virginia Chapter of the Non-Resident Nepali Association has launched a fundraiser for the surviving family members of two Fairfax stabbing victims. Police say Chhatra Thapa, 54, killed his wife, Binda Thapa, 52, and his daughter, Mamta, 33, a nurse, during a Feb. 23 attack.

City of Fairfax police arrested a juvenile accused of seriously assaulting a convenience store employee during a robbery Monday morning. Officers responded around 5 a.m. to the 7-Eleven at 3965 Jermantown Road.

Virginia lawmakers and educators are considering rules for artificial intelligence in schools as its use grows and students spend more time on screens. Del. Sam Rasoul, a Roanoke Democrat, said concerns include student safety and the technology’s effects on critical thinking and learning.

Tulip Day will bring 150,000 freshly bloomed flowers to the National Mall on Sunday, March 15. The event runs from 11:15 a.m. to 4:15 p.m., and ticket holders can pick bouquets of up to 10 tulips each.

Fairfax County transportation officials will hold a second public meeting for the Oakton Congestion and Safety Study at 7 p.m. Tuesday, March 10, in the Oakton High School cafeteria. Staff will review existing conditions and gather feedback on potential improvements developed after a November workshop.

Friends of the Mount Vernon Trail received the Public Lands Alliance’s Trail of the Year award for its renovation work. The volunteer maintenance and education group was honored at the alliance’s conference in Daytona Beach, Florida, in February.

Rain is likely Tuesday, with a 70% chance before 2 p.m. and possible showers after 5 p.m. Cloudy skies and a high near 48 degrees are expected. Rain chances rise to 80% after 7 p.m., with a low around 45 and another tenth to a quarter-inch of precipitation possible.
