A teen faces a malicious wounding charge after allegedly stabbing another teen during a fight at South Lakes Village Center in Reston on Friday, March 27. Fairfax County police said the victim was hospitalized with injuries that were not life-threatening.

Firefighters rescued a resident Monday night after a large tree fell onto a two-story home in the 10300 block of Darby Street, trapping the occupant inside. Crews from the Fairfax County and Fairfax City fire departments entered the house and helped the resident escape without injury. Units remained at the scene afterward.

In Lorton, firefighters responded early Monday to a blaze in the 10700 block of Old Colchester Road. They arrived to find a boathouse engulfed in flames, with the fire spreading into nearby brush. No injuries were reported.

Virginia hospital systems are becoming more vocal about changes stemming from last year’s One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Facilities statewide are confronting reduced federal funding and rising insurance costs after hospital chains had largely avoided discussing their concerns publicly.

The National Zoo is set to receive a record $51.6 million donation from Saudi royalty for a new Arabian leopard habitat. Construction is expected to begin in late summer, with an opening targeted for 2029.

Broad Street Realty Inc., a publicly traded Reston real estate company known for grocery-anchored shopping centers, filed for Chapter 7 bankruptcy liquidation on March 20. The filing followed a turbulent year in which the company lost control of its portfolio. Its 15 properties were taken over last year by an investor affiliated with Fortress Investment Group, which planned to market them for sale, according to regulatory filings.

Reston-based government contractor Leidos announced Monday that it completed its $2.4 billion acquisition of Illinois-based Entrust Solutions Group, a utilities infrastructure design company. The deal will effectively double Leidos’ footprint in the energy infrastructure market and add more than 3,100 Entrust employees.
