Luise Estelle is bringing a boutique approach to medical aesthetics in the Tysons–McLean corridor, combining advanced cosmetic technology with personalized treatment plans and an emphasis on discreet service.

Her practice, Tysons Elite Esthetics, focuses on what Estelle calls a “refined glow”: subtle enhancements intended to complement a client’s natural features rather than create dramatic changes.

Located at 7777 Leesburg Pike in Falls Church, the clinic is minutes from Tysons Galleria and the Silver Line Metro. It serves clients from Tysons, McLean, Vienna, Great Falls and elsewhere in Northern Virginia.

Estelle grew up in Germany, where she developed the disciplined, detail-oriented approach that now shapes her business. Before entering the aesthetics industry, she worked with luxury retailers and brands including Neiman Marcus and Dior, gaining experience in presentation, customer service and client care.

Her attention eventually turned to beauty and skin science. She began evaluating aesthetic treatments and technologies while building teams trained to perform them. At Tysons Elite Esthetics, she continues to emphasize staff education and familiarity with developing techniques.

The clinic offers nonsurgical services for concerns including signs of aging, acne scarring, pigmentation, unwanted hair and body contouring. Available treatments include Botox and Dysport, dermal fillers, collagen-stimulating procedures, medical-grade facials, chemical peels and microneedling.

Laser services include skin resurfacing, IPL photofacials and hair removal. The practice also provides radiofrequency skin tightening, ultrasound-based lifting, muscle toning and noninvasive fat-reduction treatments.

Among its equipment is CoolSculpting Elite, which uses controlled cooling for body contouring. Wonder Axon and Visage systems are offered for muscle-toning treatments targeting areas such as the abdomen and glutes.

The clinic’s Rohrer laser platform supports treatments including fractional carbon-dioxide resurfacing and IPL photofacials. Pixel8 RF Microneedling combines microneedling with radiofrequency energy in procedures intended to promote collagen production and tighten the skin.

Estelle says staffing is a central part of the practice. Team members include Virginia-licensed Master Estheticians trained to provide services such as laser treatments, chemical peels and microneedling. The clinic also operates under the medical supervision of a board-certified plastic surgeon.

Its private setting and scheduling model are designed in part for professionals working around Tysons. Some treatments can be combined in an “Executive Refresh” appointment for clients seeking several services within a limited window.

For Estelle, the Falls Church clinic represents the culmination of years spent working in luxury service and medical aesthetics. Its central premise is straightforward: use trained practitioners, individualized planning and modern equipment to pursue refreshed, natural-looking results.
