Noufal Haircolor Studio offers customized color, cutting and styling services at Westwood Center in Tysons, with an emphasis on detailed consultations and individualized results.

Owner and master stylist Irina Russu has worked in the hair industry for more than a decade. She remains active behind the chair while mentoring the salon’s team and overseeing its approach to client care.

Color is a central focus of the studio. Services include balayage, highlights, color correction and larger transformations designed around each client’s hair, preferred maintenance routine and desired appearance. Consultations cover previous treatments, lifestyle and other factors that can affect the final result.

The salon also provides precision haircuts, keratin and smoothing treatments, blowouts, formal styling, bridal hair and makeup, extensions, and volume services. Its stylists receive continuing education in advanced coloring methods and current techniques.

Curly and textured hair clients can receive consultations that consider curl pattern, density, porosity and differences in how the hair behaves when wet or dry. Available options include customized cuts, conditioning treatments and keratin services intended to work with each client’s natural texture.

Client reviews highlighted in the original account frequently praise the studio’s attentive consultations, dimensional color work and welcoming atmosphere. Russu is also repeatedly recognized for listening to clients and tailoring services to their goals.

The salon is located at 8605 Westwood Center Drive, Suite 160, in the Tysons area of Vienna. Its location is accessible from McLean, Vienna and Falls Church.
