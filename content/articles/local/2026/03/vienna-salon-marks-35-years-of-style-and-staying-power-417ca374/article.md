Salon Daniel has spent 35 years cultivating a loyal Northern Virginia clientele, growing from founder Daniel Bechara’s vision into a full-service salon near the Dunn Loring Metro station in Vienna.

Bechara, a master stylist originally from Lebanon, founded the business in 1991. Known for precision cuts, modern styling, hair extensions and volume techniques, he has worked with public figures including Liv Tyler, Steven Tyler, Arnold Schwarzenegger, Fran Drescher, Jane Seymour and Queen Noor of Jordan.

The salon also serves local professionals, families and longtime residents from Vienna, Falls Church, McLean, Arlington and elsewhere in the Washington metropolitan area. Some clients have returned for decades.

Salon Daniel offers haircuts and styling for women and men, professional color and highlights, balayage, extensions, blowouts, grooming and special-occasion services. Appointments begin with a consultation intended to clarify each client’s preferred cut, color or style.

Its stylists pursue workshops and advanced training in coloring, precision cutting, hair restoration and new styling methods. The salon aims to combine the service of an upscale beauty studio with a comfortable neighborhood atmosphere.

Over the years, Salon Daniel has received recognition from Washingtonian, Vogue, Elle and New Beauty. Customer reviews frequently mention its friendly environment, attentive stylists and personalized service.

The business has also participated in charitable events and fundraisers supporting animal welfare and community relief efforts.

Salon Daniel is located at 2750-D Gallows Road in Vienna. Its continued presence since 1991 stands out in an industry shaped by rapidly changing trends, with generations of returning clients helping sustain the salon’s Northern Virginia reputation.
