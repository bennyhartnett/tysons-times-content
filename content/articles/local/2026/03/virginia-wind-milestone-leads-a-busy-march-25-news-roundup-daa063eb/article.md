Virginia’s largest offshore wind project has begun delivering commercial power to the grid, leading a busy Wednesday news roundup that also includes police investigations, court developments and a toll-payment scam warning.

Dominion Energy switched on the first commercial turbine in its Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind project Monday. Located off Virginia Beach, the 14.7-megawatt turbine is separate from two pilot turbines operating since 2020. The development is described as the nation’s largest offshore wind project.

Virginia State Police are investigating a fatal hit-and-run on Interstate 395 in Alexandria. A pedestrian was found dead around 6:12 a.m. Tuesday on the northbound side of the highway near the Duke Street exit.

A Fairfax County man received a 10-year prison sentence for assaults and attempted abductions near Metro stations in Alexandria last year. Nine years were suspended, leaving one year to serve. He was sentenced Friday, March 20, on assault, sexual battery and attempted abduction charges.

Fairfax County police are warning residents about fraudulent text messages demanding immediate payment for supposed unpaid tolls. The messages falsely invoke the Fairfax County General District Court and threaten penalties or legal action to pressure recipients into responding.

Two Loudoun County lawmakers asked the State Corporation Commission last week to consider placing high-voltage transmission lines underground. Their request followed General Assembly approval of bills that would establish a pilot program for the proposed lines.

Some disabled Virginians under guardianship could regain the ability to vote under legislation awaiting Gov. Abigail Spanberger’s signature. The measure, led by Fairfax Del. Kathy Tran, would require courts to make a separate determination about a person’s competency to vote when imposing guardianship.

A 19-year-old Centreville man faces 20 charges tied to four armed robberies in Arlington on Feb. 24 and 25. He was arrested at his home Friday with assistance from the FBI and police departments in Fairfax County and Herndon.

Stream Realty Partners has taken over leasing duties for 8000 Towers Crescent Drive, a 481,007-square-foot office building in Tysons, according to a March 24 announcement. Tenants include Chima Steakhouse and the recently renovated Tower Club.

Wednesday’s forecast calls for partly sunny skies, a high near 59 degrees and south winds of 6 to 9 mph. Mostly cloudy conditions are expected Wednesday night, with a low around 49 and south winds of 7 to 9 mph.
