Warmer weather is bringing farmers markets back across Northern Virginia, with vendors offering locally produced fruits, vegetables, meats, eggs, cheeses, plants, baked goods and other products. Most vendors sell items they have grown or made themselves.

Here is the 2026 market schedule by day of the week.

## Wednesday

**Oakmont Farmers Market**
May 6–October 28, 8 a.m.–noon
Oakmont Rec Center
3200 Jermantown Road, Oakton

**FRESHFARM Reston**
January 7–December 30, 3–7 p.m.
Year-round market
Winter hours: 2–6 p.m., November 2026–March 2027
St. John Neumann Catholic Church
11900 Lawyers Road, Reston

## Thursday

**Herndon Farmers Market**
May 7–October 29, 8 a.m.–noon
765 Lynn St., Herndon

## Friday

**McLean Farmers Market**
May 1–October 30, 8 a.m.–noon
Closed May 15 for the McLean Day event
Lewinsville Park
1659 Chain Bridge Road, McLean

## Saturday

**Falls Church Farmers Market**
April–December, 8 a.m.–noon
January–March, 9 a.m.–noon
City Hall parking lot
300 Park Ave., Falls Church

**Leesburg Saturday Market**
May–October, 8 a.m.–noon
November–April, 9 a.m.–noon
Virginia Village Shopping Center
30 Catoctin Circle SE, Leesburg

**Reston Farmers Market**
April 25–November 21, 8 a.m.–noon
Lake Anne Village Center
1609 Washington Plaza N., Reston

**Vienna Farmers Market**
May–October, 8 a.m.–noon
Vienna Community Center
120 Cherry St. SE, Vienna

**Fairfax Saturday Farmers’ Market**
May 9–October 31, 9 a.m.–1 p.m.
10500 Page Ave., Fairfax

**EatLoco Ashbrook Farmers Market**
Year-round, 9 a.m.–1 p.m.
44505 Atwater Drive, Ashburn

**Great Falls Farmers Market**

756 Walker Road, Great Falls
