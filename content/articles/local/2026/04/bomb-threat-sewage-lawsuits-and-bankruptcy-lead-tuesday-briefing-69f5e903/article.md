A bomb threat involving a Dulles-bound flight, lawsuits over a major Potomac River sewage spill and a steakhouse company’s bankruptcy filing lead the local news for Tuesday, April 21.

A United Airlines flight from Denver landed safely at Dulles International Airport on Monday morning following a reported bomb threat. United Flight 2408 had been evacuated by airstairs before leaving Denver International Airport because of a security concern.

The U.S. Justice Department and the Maryland Department of the Environment have filed separate lawsuits against D.C. Water. The legal actions concern pollution from a ruptured pipe that released a massive amount of sewage into the Potomac River in January.

Sen. Mark Warner and his wife, Lisa Collis, announced Monday that their daughter, Madison Warner, has died. The 36-year-old had faced juvenile diabetes and other health problems for decades.

Virginia immigration advocates are offering a mixed response to Gov. Abigail Spanberger’s handling of legislation intended to address actions by Immigration and Customs Enforcement during President Donald Trump’s immigration crackdown. Spanberger signed some of the bills passed by Democratic state lawmakers, while advocates are urging legislators to reject some measures she amended.

The interpretive center at Historic Blenheim in Fairfax City will reopen Wednesday after closing for winter maintenance. It will operate Wednesdays through Saturdays from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Free guided tours of the Historic Blenheim house will be offered Saturdays at 1 p.m.

801 Restaurant Group, the parent company of 801 Chophouse, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on April 10. The company said it intends to restructure its debt and keep its restaurants open. The chain opened its first East Coast location in Tysons in 2024.

Virginia health officials are also warning residents that warmer spring weather brings increased tick activity. Ticks in the state can transmit Lyme disease, Rocky Mountain spotted fever and Alpha-gal Syndrome.

Lake Anne Plaza in Reston presented its inaugural Good Neighbor Awards on Saturday. Five-year-old Elianna Malesic received the children’s award for helping remove litter, while local artist Pat McIntyre was honored in the adult category for hosting a free crafts table every Saturday.

Tuesday’s forecast calls for widespread frost before 10 a.m., followed by sunshine and a high near 62 degrees. Calm winds will shift south at about 6 mph during the afternoon. Overnight, skies will be partly cloudy with a low around 50 and a 30% chance of showers after 2 a.m.
