U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement is using technology capable of intercepting encrypted messages as the agency works to disrupt fentanyl trafficking, acting director Todd Lyons said in a letter sent last week.

The Trump administration revived a $2 million contract last year with Paragon Solutions, an Israeli company whose U.S. branch is based in Chantilly. The contract covers use of the company’s Graphite spyware.

In Vienna, opposition is growing over the cost and need for a proposed community pool. Local officials say residents have sought a pool for decades, with demand increasing after one was omitted from renovations to the Vienna Community Center. Critics have posted lawn signs declaring, “small pool, BIG cost.”

Supporters of ranked-choice voting believe Virginia’s congressional elections this fall could strengthen their argument for changing how statewide contests are conducted. They say crowded ballots may reveal weaknesses in the winner-take-all system if voters approve a congressional redistricting constitutional amendment on April 21.

Virginia safety-net clinics serving people with HIV are under pressure after a year marked by federal funding cuts and a state health department billing error. Those problems have made access to essential medication more difficult.

ICE agents made nearly 20,000 arrests across Virginia, Maryland and Washington, D.C., from the beginning of President Donald Trump’s second administration last year through March 10. The agency recorded nearly 3,800 arrests in the region during the final full year of President Joe Biden’s administration.

Former U.S. Rep. Jennifer Wexton has endorsed former Virginia first lady Dorothy McAuliffe in her campaign for Congress. McAuliffe is seeking the seat in the proposed 7th Congressional District under the Democrats’ redistricting plan.

In Annandale, the chicken restaurant formerly called Chi Mc has been renamed K Chicken. Owner Eddie Ryu said the food remains unchanged at the restaurant, located at 6653-H Little River Turnpike in the Willow Run shopping center.

Sisters Tysons is marking Songkran, the Thai New Year, with weekly Friday events throughout April. The Capital One Center restaurant will pair a family-style Thai meal with live traditional dance performances.

Wednesday will be sunny with a high near 54 degrees and a northeast wind around 6 mph. Mostly clear skies and a low near 35 are expected overnight, with frost possible after 3 a.m. and southeast winds of 3 to 6 mph.
