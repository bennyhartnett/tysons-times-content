Metro is considering a $93 million-plus overhaul of its aging fare-vending system, while drivers in Tysons face overnight Dulles Toll Road closures this weekend.

The Metro board is scheduled to vote Thursday on a proposal to purchase and install new fare machines. Beyond selling fares, the replacements could help riders plan trips and receive service alerts.

All eastbound lanes of the Dulles Toll Road will close between Route 7 and the Capital Beltway beginning around Friday night, April 10. The nightly closures and detours will continue through Sunday night, April 12, to allow overhead sign work for the 495 NEXT project.

Gov. Abigail Spanberger is also answering criticism from fellow Democrats who say she has not done enough publicly to promote Virginia’s April 21 redistricting referendum. Spanberger said Wednesday that she has clearly expressed her support while concentrating on governing during her administration’s opening months.

A Great Falls father and son made an unusually long trip to see two of the NBA’s lowest-ranked teams play Sunday. Erin-Michael Gill and his son Nicholas attended Easter Mass at 7 a.m., then drove four and a half hours to Barclays Center in New York for the Washington Wizards’ 3:30 p.m. game against the Brooklyn Nets. They joined hundreds of spectators attending the Kids’ Day matchup.

Along the George Washington Memorial Parkway, a major portion of the long-running repaving project is nearing completion. The National Park Service is now considering ways to restore signature views at 15 locations along a 2.65-mile stretch of the road.

Underdog Coffee & Bread is preparing to expand about a year after opening its Columbia Pike cafe in Annandale. Its second location is planned for Carlin Springs Road in Bailey’s Crossroads, next to REI.

Vienna families can visit the Parks and Recreation Department’s Wonder Wagon at the Patrick Henry Library’s temporary location Friday. The mobile nature center will be at 262 Cedar Lane in Cedar Park Shopping Center from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m.

Clay Cafe Studios in Falls Church has a new appearance and owner. Jessi Cash, who has worked at the paint-your-own-pottery business since 2019, took over from retiring owner Gayla Hassett in January.

A newly filed legal notice seeks an on- and off-premises license for mixed beverages, wine and beer.

Thursday will be mostly sunny, with a high near 62 degrees. Calm winds are expected to shift southeast at about 6 mph during the afternoon. The night should remain mostly clear, with a low around 42 and southeast winds of 3 to 6 mph.
