A four-bedroom home with direct access to the W&OD Trail is listed for $1.325 million at 10305 Mountington Court in Reston’s Tamarack neighborhood.

The 3,615-square-foot St. Clair model sits on a cul-de-sac and includes two full bathrooms, one half-bath and an attached two-car garage. Built in 1972, the detached home also comes with a Hunter Mill Swim and Racquet Club membership.

Brazilian cherry hardwood floors extend from the foyer throughout the main level. The living room features plantation shutters and connects to a dining room overlooking the flat backyard.

The eat-in kitchen has granite countertops, newer stainless steel appliances and extensive cabinet storage. A sliding glass door opens to a Trex deck, patio and yard. The main floor also includes a powder room, laundry and mudroom, and a family room with plantation shutters and a wood-burning fireplace framed by brick and a large custom wood mantel.

Upstairs, the primary suite offers two closets, including a walk-in closet with organizers. Its private bathroom has a walk-in shower and double vanity. Three additional bedrooms have ceiling fans, bright windows and custom California Closets, with access to a shared hall bathroom with a tub.

The finished lower level provides a large carpeted recreation area with recessed lighting. Four full-size windows bring in natural light, and a door leads directly to the yard.

Outside, the property includes a level backyard, patio and playset that will remain with the home. The house also has a new roof.
