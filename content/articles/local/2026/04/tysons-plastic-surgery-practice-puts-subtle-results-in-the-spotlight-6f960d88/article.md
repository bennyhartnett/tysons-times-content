Dr. Munique Maia has built a boutique cosmetic surgery practice in Tysons focused on personalized care and natural-looking results.

Rather than pursuing dramatic transformations, Maia Plastic Surgery emphasizes refinement. Its stated goal is to help patients appear rested and refreshed while preserving their recognizable features.

Maia is a Harvard fellowship-trained plastic surgeon whose academic work includes more than 50 scientific articles, abstracts and book chapters. She has also contributed to and edited surgical publications, with research centered partly on facial rejuvenation and breast surgery.

Her professional honors include an Outstanding Paper Award from the American Society of Plastic Surgeons and a Best Paper Award for cosmetic breast surgery research. She has also received recognition from regional publications including Washingtonian, Northern Virginia Magazine and Modern Luxury DC.

## Facial, breast and body procedures

The practice offers facelifts, mini-lifts, neck lifts, jawline contouring, eyelid surgery and fat grafting for facial volume restoration.

Breast procedures include augmentation, lifts, implant removal and revision surgery, as well as treatment of asymmetry and cases involving medical concerns.

Body-contouring services include tummy tucks, liposuction, sculpting and procedures for patients who have experienced significant weight loss. Nonsurgical options include Botox, dermal fillers and skin-rejuvenation treatments.

The practice describes its approach as conservative and customized, with recommendations based on each patient’s goals.

## A concierge-style experience

Patient reviews frequently highlight detailed consultations, realistic expectations and continued communication after surgery. Other recurring themes include short wait times, individualized attention and support from an all-female team.

Published reviews also commonly describe natural-looking results, manageable recoveries and candid recommendations. Some patients report traveling considerable distances for treatment.

Maia Plastic Surgery is located at 8100 Boone Blvd., Suite 730, in Tysons.
