Greenhouse Kitchen + Bar combines international dining, live performances and late-night dancing in Vienna’s Tysons Corner corridor.

The restaurant at 2070 Chain Bridge Road features a contemporary, greenhouse-inspired interior with botanical details and warm lighting. Its menu draws primarily from Mediterranean and Latin cuisines while incorporating flavors from other culinary traditions.

Offerings include lamb chops, tuna tartare, sushi rolls, Persian-inspired soups, butter chicken and Chicken Kabob Pizza. Cocktails are also part of the menu.

From 4 to 7 p.m. Monday through Friday, Greenhouse advertises a free all-you-can-eat happy hour with rotating selections such as flatbreads, wings, sliders, dips and salads.

Music and cultural programming are central to the venue. Events have included Spanish guitar, flamenco, Arabic pop, Latin house, Afrobeat and Persian Nowruz celebrations. Regular offerings include live Latin performances and Live Flamenco Saturdays.

Guests can move between two musical settings: a main dining room featuring live performers and the separate O-Lounge, where DJs continue into the early morning on some nights. The restaurant also stages a high-energy Sunday brunch with musicians, DJs and dancers.

Greenhouse hosts private gatherings ranging from birthday and heritage celebrations to memorial events. Its programming is designed to appeal to Fairfax County’s multicultural community, with Arabic, Persian, Latin and other international influences represented throughout its calendar.

The restaurant has received a Tripadvisor Travelers’ Choice Award, a distinction given to businesses that rank among the platform’s top 10% of listings. It has also accumulated more than 1,800 Google reviews with an average rating of four stars.

Greenhouse is open from 4 to 10 p.m. Wednesday, 4 p.m. to midnight Thursday, 4 p.m. to 2 a.m. Friday, 5 p.m. to 2 a.m. Saturday and noon to 11 p.m. Sunday. It is closed Monday and Tuesday, despite advertising its happy-hour promotion for weekdays.

Weekend reservations are recommended. The venue says it strictly enforces a dress code on event nights, and valet parking is available.
