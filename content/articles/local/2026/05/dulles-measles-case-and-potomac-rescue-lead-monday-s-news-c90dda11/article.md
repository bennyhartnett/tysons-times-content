A kayaker suffered life-threatening injuries Sunday after falling into the Potomac River and being swept away near Great Falls. Montgomery County Fire and Rescue spokesperson Pete Piringer said three men were involved in the incident.

Health officials in Virginia and Washington, D.C., have confirmed a regional measles case involving a D.C. resident who traveled through Washington Dulles International Airport on April 23 and April 24.

A second lawsuit filed in Tazewell County Circuit Court is challenging a proposed Virginia constitutional amendment that would protect reproductive rights. The complaint alleges that the ballot language is misleading and raises arguments similar to an earlier case disputing the legality of the amendment process.

Attorneys for Brendan Banfield have asked a court to set aside his guilty verdict in the “au pair affair” double-murder trial. Banfield faces a mandatory life sentence, and arguments on the motion are expected during his sentencing hearing at 11 a.m. Friday, May 8.

Washington-area residents could face at least two weeks of very high tree and grass pollen levels, prolonging a difficult stretch of spring allergy symptoms.

Weekday lane closures are scheduled to continue along Route 29 between Union Mill Road/Centreville Farms Drive and Summit Drive/Buckleys Gate Drive from May 4 through May 15, weather permitting. The closures are part of the Route 29 widening project, and drivers should expect delays or consider alternate routes.

Virginia has received an additional month to comply with a federal court order restoring voting rights to residents with certain felony convictions. The judge said the state’s longstanding disenfranchisement practices have violated federal law for more than a century.

Fairfax County residents can pick up as many as three free native tree or shrub seedlings at the county’s Interstate 66 Transfer Station and Interstate 95 Landfill Complex. Distribution began May 1.

Public budget hearings are scheduled for 6 p.m. May 12, with May 13 reserved as an overflow date. The proceedings will be broadcast.

Monday will be mostly sunny, with a high near 76 degrees. Southwest winds of 7 to 11 mph could gust as high as 20 mph. Partly cloudy conditions are expected overnight, with a low around 61.
