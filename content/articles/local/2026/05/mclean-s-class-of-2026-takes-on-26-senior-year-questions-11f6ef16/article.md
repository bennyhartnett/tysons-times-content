McLean seniors are being invited to reflect on their high school experience through 26 questions about favorite memories, difficult challenges, teachers, traditions and graduation.

1. What advice would you offer your freshman-year self?
2. Which school year would you choose to experience again?
3. What new class would you design specifically for seniors?
4. What is your favorite memory from high school?
5. Which three teachers would you take on a Himalayan hike?
6. If you became a teacher, what subject would you teach?
7. Which song should play as you cross the graduation stage?
8. What was the best theater production?
9. Which club should every student consider joining?
10. What was senior year’s worst “brain rot”?
11. Which high school trend was the best?
12. What was your most effective excuse for leaving class?
13. What was the greatest challenge you overcame in high school?
14. Who was your favorite teacher, and what made that person stand out?
15. What late-night snack helped you through study sessions?
16. What was your most embarrassing high school moment?
17. What will you miss most about McLean?
18. What was the wildest thing you witnessed in a McLean hallway?
19. If 2026 were a chapter in your life story, what would it be called?
20. What is your unpopular opinion about high school?
21. Where was the best place to spend time with friends?
22. Which classic television high school most resembles McLean?
23. When did you experience your worst fear of missing out?
24. Which school event was your favorite?
25. If you were alone at school overnight, what would you do first?
26. Which single day from high school would you choose to relive?
