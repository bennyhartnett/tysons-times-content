Fairfax County transportation officials are planning a series of pedestrian safety and connectivity upgrades along Pohick Road, covering the corridor between Route 1 and the bridge over Interstate 95.

The proposed project would add new asphalt and concrete walkway segments, helping fill gaps in the pedestrian network. Plans also call for relocating a signalized pedestrian crossing and installing curb and gutter throughout the corridor.

The Fairfax County Department of Transportation will present the proposal during a virtual community information meeting on Tuesday, May 26, at 7 p.m. The online session will cover the project’s purpose and need, the improvements under consideration, the anticipated schedule and the next steps in the process.

Residents can join through Microsoft Teams on the web using meeting ID 286 465 214 169 707 and passcode rm3qe6EZ. Participants may also call 571-429-5982 and enter phone conference ID 351 822 008#. Additional details are available on the county’s Pohick Road Pedestrian Project webpage.
