A fully renovated townhouse at 125 Carter Court SW in the Town of Vienna is on the market for $1.05 million.

Built in 1994, the 2,280-square-foot home has three bedrooms, three full bathrooms and one half-bath. Renovations completed over the past five years include a new kitchen and bathrooms in 2021, a Trex deck in 2022, and new HVAC and water-heating systems in 2024. The roof was replaced in 2020.

The kitchen features navy-blue cabinetry, white quartz countertops and stainless-steel appliances. Other details include hardwood floors, 10-foot ceilings and a deck.

Upstairs, the vaulted primary bedroom has a walk-in closet and a renovated bathroom with a large walk-in shower and double vanity. Laundry is also located on the bedroom level.

The walkout basement opens to a flagstone patio, and the property includes a two-car garage. The home is within walking distance of Maple Avenue and downtown Vienna and is about one mile from the Vienna Metro station.
