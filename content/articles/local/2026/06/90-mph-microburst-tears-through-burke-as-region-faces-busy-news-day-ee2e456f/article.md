A powerful microburst packing 90 mph winds struck Burke during severe storms Monday evening, June 22. National Weather Service teams surveyed the damage Tuesday and determined that the burst stretched about one mile long and 500 feet wide. The storm knocked down trees and damaged property, including a fence hit by a falling tree.

In Annandale, Fairfax County police arrested a suspect accused of exposing his genitals before attempting to grab a woman and pull her into the woods. Police said the suspect was in custody and there was no continuing threat to the community.

Military aircraft will fly near the National Mall from Wednesday, June 24, through Friday, July 10, for events marking America’s 250th anniversary. The flyovers coincide with the Great American State Fair, a 16-day exposition organized by Freedom 250 and scheduled for June 25 through July 10.

The ACLU filed a motion June 18 seeking faster relief for thousands of Virginians with certain past felony convictions who remain unable to complete voter registration. The civil rights group accused election officials of violating the outcome of a voting-rights lawsuit decided earlier this year.

Virginia’s Department of Social Services has halted a two-year information technology project intended to replace the state’s aging child-support enforcement system. Millions of dollars had already been spent on the effort. Officials must now determine what to do with a system that was considered antiquated as far back as 1998.

Fairfax City’s Office of Elections has finalized the candidates who qualified for city races in the Nov. 3 general and special election. Incumbent City Council member Tom Peterson will face Kirsten Sides Lockhart in the mayoral contest after Mayor Catherine Read announced that she would not seek reelection.

Michael “Mac” Cummins, who resigned as Reston Association CEO in January, has been selected to lead Austin Economic Development. Austin announced his appointment June 16, and he is scheduled to begin the new job July 27.

A six-bedroom, eight-bathroom house near Scott’s Run Nature Preserve is on the market for nearly $6 million. The custom home at 938 Saigon Road in McLean sits on 1.4 acres and includes an elevator, home theater, golf simulator and patio with a fire pit.

Wednesday’s forecast calls for sunshine, a high near 81 degrees and a northwest wind around 7 mph. Skies should remain mostly clear overnight as the temperature falls to about 62 degrees and winds become calm.
