Northern Virginia faces a sweltering Friday, with temperatures near 95 degrees and a heat index potentially reaching 103. Showers and thunderstorms are 80% likely, mainly after 5 p.m., and some storms could become severe. Storms may continue until about 8 p.m., followed by an overnight low near 67.

Virginia lawmakers, meanwhile, questioned Dominion Energy leaders in Richmond this week about a proposed $67 billion all-stock merger with NextEra. The deal could shift management of half of Virginia’s energy operations outside the commonwealth. Fairfax Del. Rip Sullivan and Senate Majority Leader Scott Surovell were among the Virginia Energy Commission members scrutinizing the acquisition.

At the Kennedy Center, the board selected by President Donald Trump voted Thursday to seek a stay of a May 29 court ruling that found Trump’s name had been placed on the building illegally. The move is an attempt to keep the name on the performing arts center’s facade ahead of a Friday removal deadline.

A Virginia law taking effect July 1 will seal criminal records for many people convicted of low-level offenses. Supporters say it could improve access to jobs and housing years after an offense, while critics have expressed concerns about public safety.

Doña Mesa Tex Mex Kitchen opened Thursday at 106 Braddock Road, Suite C, in University Mall near George Mason University. Its menu includes tacos, fajitas and quesadillas, along with Mexican-style ribs, stuffed shrimp, marinated grilled chicken and ribeye steak. The restaurant joins recent arrivals including Another Broken Egg Cafe and the renamed Velocity Bar and Kitchen.

The Fairfax County Board of Supervisors approved a zoning special exception June 9 allowing a major expansion of the private school for elite student-athletes at the St. James sports complex in Springfield. The St. James Performance Academy will be permitted to enroll 660 students and add 110 employees.

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is underway through July 19, with watch parties planned throughout Fairfax County. Tysons is among the locations where fans can gather for U.S. Men’s National Team matches on June 12, June 19 and June 25.

Springfield Town Center will host the eighth annual Taste of Springfield Festival on Saturday, June 13, from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. The family-friendly event, held in the parking lot near Dick’s Sporting Goods, will feature dozens of outdoor exhibitors and food from local restaurants and chefs.

Fairfax County Supervisor Walter Alcorn will hold a community town hall on the Reston Town Center North rezoning proposal Tuesday, June 16, at 6:30 p.m. at the North County Governmental Center. The proposal is expected to advance to the Planning Commission in July.

A new public notice also announces that an establishment is seeking a restaurant alcoholic beverage control license. Any objections must be submitted within 30 days.
