Emergency crews rescued a driver from a pond near Walney Road and Westfields Boulevard in Chantilly after her vehicle crashed into the water. Fairfax County firefighters and police formed a human chain to bring her safely to shore. She was treated for minor injuries.

Here are other developments for Monday, June 29, 2026:

The approximately 2.5-mile 495 Express Lanes Extension Bicycle and Pedestrian Trail is now open. VDOT and Transurban marked the opening Saturday with a ribbon-cutting ceremony and family fitness events attended by hundreds of community members. The celebration took place in the future Scott’s Run Nature Preserve annex parking lot.

United Airlines is supporting a proposal to accelerate renovations at Dulles International Airport. Company executive Steve Morrissey said discussions involving United, the U.S. Department of Transportation and the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority were underway in earnest, describing the faster overhaul as the Trump administration’s preferred option.

Venezuelan restaurants across Northern Virginia, Maryland and Washington are organizing donations for earthquake relief in a South American country where more than 1,400 people have died and over 3,000 have been injured. Large search-and-rescue efforts are also underway.

Virginia is preparing to require more disclosure about data centers’ water consumption. Regulations taking effect in January will provide additional information about industry usage, although figures for individual facilities will remain protected as trade secrets. A state budget amendment seeks further reporting requirements.

Higher education leaders in Virginia and Ohio announced Thursday that they will collaborate on a model for three-year bachelor’s degrees, potentially shortening the traditional undergraduate timeline by one year.

The Virginia ABC store at 6343 Columbia Pike in Barcroft Plaza will close permanently after July 12. The Lincolnia area will continue to have an ABC location at Landmark Plaza, 6206 Little River Turnpike.

Historic Blenheim is closed from June 29 through July 8 while the City of Fairfax renovates the lobby of the modern Civil War Interpretive Center. The grounds remain open, including goat viewing, and house tours are scheduled to resume after the work is finished.

A public hearing on school boundaries is scheduled for 6 p.m. July 13, 2026.

Monday’s forecast calls for patchy fog before 9 a.m., followed by partly sunny skies and a high near 86. Northeast winds will range from 3 to 6 mph. The night should be mostly clear with a low around 67 and southeast winds of 3 to 5 mph.
