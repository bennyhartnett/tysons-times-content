Fairfax County transportation officials will hold a virtual public meeting entirely in Spanish on Wednesday, June 10, 2026, to review the Route 29 Active Transportation Study and its proposed improvements. The meeting begins at 7 p.m.

Participants can join through Microsoft Teams using meeting ID **211 251 512 593 516** and passcode **ZS3QM6sV**. They may also call **571-429-5982** and enter phone conference ID **409 373 692#**.

The Fairfax County Department of Transportation is seeking public feedback on the proposed transportation changes. Presentation slides and a survey are available online.

Comments can also be submitted by calling **703-890-5898**, entering project code **2402** and leaving a recorded voicemail. Feedback will be accepted through the close of business on Tuesday, June 30, 2026.
