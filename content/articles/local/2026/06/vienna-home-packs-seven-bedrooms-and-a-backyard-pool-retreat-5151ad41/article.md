A seven-bedroom custom home with a pool and pool house is on the market in Vienna Woods for $1,999,999.

Built in 2023, the Craftsman-style residence at 1114 Ware Street SW offers 4,844 square feet of living space on a quiet 0.29-acre lot. It has five full bathrooms and one half-bath.

Inside, the home features high ceilings, hardwood floors, designer lighting and an open layout. The dining room connects to the family room, while the kitchen includes premium cabinetry, high-end appliances, double sinks, a large center island and a breakfast area with backyard access.

A bedroom with an en-suite bathroom on the main level can accommodate guests or multigenerational living.

The owner’s suite upstairs has two walk-in closets and a bathroom with double vanities, a soaking tub and a custom-tiled walk-in shower. Three more bedrooms and two full bathrooms, including a Jack-and-Jill bathroom, complete the upper floor.

The finished lower level includes a recreation room, a room that can serve as a gym or bedroom, a bonus room and another bedroom with an en-suite bathroom.

Outside, the fenced backyard features a swimming pool with a retractable cover, a large patio and a pool house containing a recreation area, kitchenette and full bathroom.

The property is near Southside Park, Yeonas Park, downtown Vienna, the W&OD Trail, Tysons Corner and Metro service. It is also within the Madison High School pyramid.

An open house is scheduled for Saturday, June 27, from noon to 2 p.m.
