Virginia lawmakers approved a two-year spending plan Monday, ending months of negotiations as a deadline to prevent a partial government shutdown approached. The Senate passed the budget 23-16, followed by a 71-22 vote in the House of Delegates. The plan now goes to Gov. Abigail Spanberger.

A Falls Church-area family and a large group of friends are mourning Sean King, who was struck while walking home from work. A car jumped the curb along a Leesburg Pike service road and hit King on the sidewalk. He died shortly afterward at a hospital.

State and local officials say data centers would face the same treatment as commercial, industrial and residential water customers if continued drought leads to mandatory restrictions.

Health officials have confirmed measles in a Maryland resident who passed through Dulles International Airport. The person traveled through Concourse C, the International Arrivals Building and baggage-claim areas between 6 and 11 a.m. on June 17 before visiting an urgent-care office in the District.

Metro awarded Indra Group a $38.9 million base contract Monday to design, produce, install and maintain 450 new customer terminal machines. The equipment will be deployed across 98 stations and at WMATA testing and training facilities. The machines will have touch screens and accept mobile wallets, credit cards and debit cards.

Northern Virginia’s supply of top-tier office space is shrinking while new construction remains limited. Real estate experts say a sufficiently large or lucrative tenant would be needed to spur another project. Comstock Chief Marketing Officer Timothy Steffan estimated that annual rents of $85 per square foot would be necessary to cover skilled labor, materials and other construction costs for a new trophy office building.

Reston residents can attend a town hall Thursday about a proposed Fairfax County Comprehensive Plan amendment involving the Crescent Apartments property near Lake Anne. The amendment could make room for a larger Reston Community Center presence in the property’s future redevelopment.

Two Fairfax County students performed on Broadway Monday night during the National High School Musical Theatre Awards, known as the Jimmy Awards. Recent Oakton High School graduate John Poncy and rising Madison High School senior Allie Blanchet appeared in character medleys after winning best leading actor and actress at the D.C. region’s Brandon Victor Dixon Awards.

A new novel released June 23 turns the Metro into a time-travel portal. *Retro*, by D.C. journalist Jessica M. Goldstein, follows Ash, an unemployed actress who joins a time-travel startup that allows wealthy customers to party in the past.

Tuesday’s forecast calls for a 40% chance of showers before 3 p.m., mostly cloudy skies and a high near 80 degrees. Conditions should become mostly clear overnight, with a low around 62 and a gentle north wind near 6 mph.
