Drivers in Falls Church should expect road closures and parking restrictions during two events on Saturday, June 6, and Sunday, June 7, 2026.

For the West Falls Summer Bash on Saturday, restrictions will run from 7 a.m. until approximately 4:30 p.m. The closures include:

- West Falls Station Boulevard from Cardinal Lane to Mustang Alley
- Portions of Husky Street from West Falls Station Boulevard to Arbor Way
- Magnolia Street from Redbud Road to West Falls Station Boulevard

Parking garages along Husky and Magnolia streets will remain accessible.

The Saint James Corpus Christi Procession will bring additional closures Sunday from 12:30 to approximately 1:45 p.m. The affected streets include:

- North Spring Street from West Broad Street to Park Avenue
- Park Avenue from North Spring Street to North Oak Street
- Portions of North Oak Street from West Broad Street to Park Avenue

City police and sheriff’s officials asked the public to cooperate with the restrictions to help protect event participants and volunteers.
