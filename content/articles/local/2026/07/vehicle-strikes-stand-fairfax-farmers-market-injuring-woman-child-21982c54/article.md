A vehicle struck a stand at a Fairfax farmers market around 11 a.m. July 25, injuring a woman and a child and prompting a police investigation in a parking lot near the Main Street Center.

Fairfax County police said the driver, described as an older woman, confused the accelerator with the brake before the crash. The supplied information does not include the driver’s name or say whether she received a citation or faced charges.

The woman and child were taken to a hospital with injuries described as non-life-threatening. The driver was not injured.

The farmers market remained open as police continued investigating the crash. The supplied account does not identify the injured woman or child, and it does not provide additional details about the stand or the circumstances surrounding the collision.
