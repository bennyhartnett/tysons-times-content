A driver traveling the wrong way in the reversible I-395 Express Lanes collided with a Virginia State Police cruiser and another vehicle in Alexandria on July 10, according to state police.

The incident began at 7:37 p.m., when Virginia State Police received a report of a vehicle traveling northbound in the wrong direction on I-95 in Prince William County. The reversible lanes were operating southbound at the time.

A trooper tracked the reported vehicle from Prince William County to I-395 through Alexandria. The trooper then entered the express lanes from the north, where the vehicles collided at approximately 7:46 p.m.

After striking the police cruiser, the suspected wrong-way vehicle hit another vehicle before coming to rest. The crash involved three vehicles in all: the suspected wrong-way vehicle, the state police cruiser and the third vehicle.

The suspected wrong-way driver was arrested at the scene and taken to a hospital for evaluation, state police said. The trooper was also taken to a hospital, treated and released. The supplied account did not report the condition of anyone in the third vehicle.

Crash debris reached the main highway, and the express lanes remained closed for hours Thursday night. The disruption reduced traffic on I-95 to two lanes in each direction.

Virginia State Police said “the incident remains under investigation.” The statement cited in the supplied account did not identify charges or provide the suspected driver’s identity.

The precise crash location beyond the I-395 Express Lanes through Alexandria was not provided. The incident details were attributed to a Virginia State Police statement cited by the supplied source.
