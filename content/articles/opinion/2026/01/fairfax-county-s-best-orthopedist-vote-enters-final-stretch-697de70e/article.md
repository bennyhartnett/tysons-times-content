Fairfax County residents have two weeks left to help choose the area’s best orthopedist in the Readers’ Choice Awards.

The poll highlights specialists who treat conditions ranging from pulled muscles to arthritis—problems that can make everyday activities difficult when they are not properly addressed. Voters can select one of the nominated Fairfax County doctors or submit the name of another orthopedist as a write-in candidate.

Voting in the orthopedist category will remain open until 8:30 a.m. Friday, Jan. 30.

A separate Readers’ Choice poll for the best pediatrician in Fairfax County is also underway, but its deadline arrives sooner. Ballots in that category must be submitted by 8:30 a.m. Friday, Jan. 23.

Results have also been finalized in an earlier poll asking readers to identify Fairfax County’s best sledding destination. Wolf Trap National Park topped that list, followed by Mount Reston and King Kong Hill.
