Fairfax County readers have two weeks to choose their favorite local barbershop in the Best Barbershop in Fairfax poll.

The contest invites voters to select from the nominated shops or submit a write-in choice if their preferred barber is not listed. Voting is scheduled to close at 8:30 a.m. Friday, Feb. 27.

The barbershop category highlights the county’s range of options for residents seeking a new haircut, whether they prioritize a particular style or a certain shop atmosphere. The archived notice does not identify the individual barbershop nominees.

Another Readers’ Choice contest remains underway for Best Nail Salon in Fairfax County. Ballots in that category must be submitted before 8:30 a.m. Friday, Feb. 20.

Results have also been announced from the Best Hair Salon in Fairfax vote held two weeks earlier. The official results listed Ersin Secilmis, Jon David Salon and the Great Clips location in Reston.
