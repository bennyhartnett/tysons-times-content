Fairfax County residents can now vote for their favorite local nail salon in the Readers’ Choice Awards.

The contest highlights salons offering professional nail services across the county, from basic trimming to full manicures and pedicures. Readers can select one of the nominated businesses or submit a write-in choice if their preferred salon does not appear among the options.

Voting in the Best Nail Salon in Fairfax County contest will remain open for two weeks. Ballots must be submitted by 8:30 a.m. Friday, Feb. 20.

A separate vote for the Best Hair Salon in Fairfax County is also underway, but that contest closes sooner. Readers have until 8:30 a.m. Friday, Feb. 13, to make their selection.

Results have also been finalized in the earlier Best Urgent Care in Fairfax County vote. Millennium Medical Care placed first, followed by Prima Urgent Care and Inova-GoHealth Urgent Care.
