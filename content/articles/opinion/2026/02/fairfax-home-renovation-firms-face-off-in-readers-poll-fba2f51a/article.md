Fairfax County residents have two weeks to choose their favorite home renovation firm in a readers’ choice poll highlighting local contractors.

The category covers businesses that handle projects ranging from major home additions to single-room remodels. Readers can vote for one of the nominated firms or submit a write-in choice if their preferred business does not appear among the candidates.

Voting in the home renovation category will remain open until 8:30 a.m. Friday, March 13.

A separate poll seeking Fairfax County’s best bicycle shop is also underway. Ballots for that category must be submitted before voting closes at 8:30 a.m. Friday, March 6.

Meanwhile, final results have been announced for the best barbershop poll conducted two weeks earlier. Great Clips in Fairfax finished first, followed by Andy’s Barber Shop and The Standard Barbershop.
