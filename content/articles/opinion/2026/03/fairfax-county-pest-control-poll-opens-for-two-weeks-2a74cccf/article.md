Fairfax County residents have two weeks to choose the area’s best pest control company in a Readers’ Choice Awards poll.

The contest highlights local professionals who remove crawling, flying and scurrying pests from homes and businesses. Voters can select a nominated company or submit a write-in choice if their preferred business is not listed.

Voting in the pest control category will remain open until 8:30 a.m. Friday, April 10.

A separate poll for the Best Lawn Care Service in Fairfax County is also underway. Ballots for that category must be submitted before voting closes at 8:30 a.m. Friday, April 3.

Results have also been finalized for the Best Landscapers in Fairfax County poll held two weeks earlier. Wheat’s Landscape took the top spot, followed by Craig’s Lawn Care in second place and Independence Landscape in third.
