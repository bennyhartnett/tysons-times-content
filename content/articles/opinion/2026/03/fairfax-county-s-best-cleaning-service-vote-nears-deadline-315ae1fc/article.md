Fairfax County residents have two weeks to vote for their favorite local cleaning service as spring approaches and seasonal cleaning moves onto the agenda for homes and businesses.

The contest features a list of nominees for Best Cleaning Service in Fairfax County. Voters whose preferred business does not appear among the choices can submit that company as a write-in candidate.

The cleaning service poll will remain open until 8:30 a.m. Friday, March 20. Residents interested in participating must cast their ballots before that deadline.

Voting is also continuing in a separate contest for Best Home Renovation Firm in Fairfax County. That poll closes one week earlier, at 8:30 a.m. Friday, March 13.

Meanwhile, final results have been announced from the Best Bicycle Shop vote held two weeks earlier. The Bike Lane took the top spot, Green Lizard Cycling placed second, and Bikenetic finished third.
