Fairfax County residents have two weeks to choose their favorite local landscaping business as spring approaches and greenery begins returning to yards and gardens across the area.

The Readers’ Choice Awards poll features a list of nominees for Best Landscaper in Fairfax County. Voters who do not see their preferred business among the candidates can submit a write-in selection.

Voting in the landscaping category will remain open until 8:30 a.m. Friday, March 27.

Residents can also continue voting for the Best Cleaning Service in Fairfax County, although that contest has an earlier deadline. Ballots for the cleaning-service category must be submitted by 8:30 a.m. Friday, March 20.

Results are already official in another home-services category. Synergy Design and Construction finished first in the vote for Best Home Renovation Firm in Fairfax. Peerless Construction placed second, followed by Ryan Reph Remodeling in third. That poll was conducted two weeks before the landscaping contest was announced.
