Fairfax County residents have two weeks to vote for their favorite lawn care service, with the poll scheduled to close at 8:30 a.m. Friday, April 3.

The nominees include companies offering common yard-maintenance services throughout the county, from mowing grass and pulling weeds to spreading mulch. Readers who do not see their preferred business among the listed choices may submit a write-in vote.

Another local poll seeking Fairfax County’s best landscaper remains open, but voting in that category ends earlier. Its deadline is 8:30 a.m. Friday, March 27.

The results of a cleaning-service poll held two weeks earlier have also been finalized. Superb Maids DC took the top spot in that vote. The Maids in Fairfax placed second, while Maid Bright finished third.
