Fairfax County families can now vote for the area’s best playground as warmer weather brings strollers and outdoor outings back into the daily routine.

The Readers’ Choice poll features a range of county playgrounds, including fully accessible destination parks and shaded neighborhood play areas tucked among the woods. Voters can select one of the listed nominees or write in a favorite that did not make the ballot.

Voting remains open for two weeks and will close at 8:30 a.m. Friday, April 17.

A separate poll for Fairfax County’s best pest control company is also underway. Ballots for that category must be submitted by 8:30 a.m. Friday, April 10.

Results are already final in the earlier vote for the best lawn care service around Fairfax. Craig’s Lawn Care finished first, followed by Wheat’s Landscape and Independence Landscape.
