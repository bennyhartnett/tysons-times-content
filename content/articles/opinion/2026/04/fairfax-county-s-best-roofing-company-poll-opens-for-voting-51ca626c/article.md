Voting is underway to choose the best roofing company in Fairfax County as part of a local Readers’ Choice Awards contest.

The poll features nominated roofing professionals serving the county, where homeowners can turn for help with leaking or deteriorating roofs. Voters may also write in a favorite business if it does not appear among the listed nominees.

The roofing company poll will remain open for two weeks. Ballots must be submitted before 8:30 a.m. on Friday, April 24.

A separate vote for the best playground in Fairfax County is also continuing. That contest closes one week earlier, at 8:30 a.m. on Friday, April 17.

Meanwhile, final results have been announced from the best pest control company poll held two weeks earlier. My Pest Pros finished first, followed by Holiday Termite & Pest Control in second place and Rossi Pest Services in third.
