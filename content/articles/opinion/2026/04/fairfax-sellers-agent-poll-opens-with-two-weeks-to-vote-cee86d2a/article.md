Fairfax County readers have two weeks to choose their favorite real estate professional for sellers in the Readers’ Choice Awards.

The poll features nominees who help clients sell homes and businesses, with experience in negotiations aimed at securing the best possible return. Readers whose preferred agent is not listed may submit a write-in candidate.

Voting for “Best Real Estate Agent for Sellers in Fairfax” will remain open until 8:30 a.m. Friday, May 8.

A separate contest for the best moving company in Fairfax County is also underway. Ballots in that category must be submitted by 8:30 a.m. Friday, May 1.

Meanwhile, the results of the county’s best roofing company poll, held two weeks earlier, are official. Pond Roofing & Exteriors finished first, followed by St. Joseph’s Roofing and Marshall Roofing.
