Fairfax County residents have until 8:30 a.m. Friday, May 15, to vote for the area’s best title company in the Readers’ Choice Awards.

The poll focuses on businesses that help property buyers navigate research and financial steps during a home or other real estate purchase. Voters can select a nominated company or submit a write-in choice if their preferred business is not listed. Voting will remain open for two weeks before the winner is determined.

A separate vote for Fairfax County’s best real estate agent for sellers is also underway, but its deadline arrives sooner. Ballots in that category must be submitted by 8:30 a.m. Friday, May 8.

Meanwhile, the results of the county’s best moving company poll, conducted two weeks earlier, are now official. JK Moving Services finished first, followed by VanGuard Moving Express in second place and Bookstore Movers in third.
