Fairfax County residents have two weeks to choose the area’s best real estate agent for buyers, with write-in candidates welcome alongside the listed nominees.

The poll focuses on agents who help buyers navigate the local housing market, whether they are purchasing their first home or relocating to Fairfax County. Voters can consider which agent is best at helping clients find properties suited to their needs and price range.

Ballots must be submitted by 8:30 a.m. Friday, July 10. Anyone whose preferred real estate agent does not appear among the nominees may add that person as a write-in selection.

Voting also remains open in a separate poll for the best golf course in Fairfax County. That contest closes at 8:30 a.m. Friday, July 3.

Results have meanwhile been finalized for the earlier best wedding venue poll. Paradise Springs Winery placed first, followed by the Atrium at Meadowlark Botanical Gardens and L’Auberge Chez Francois.
