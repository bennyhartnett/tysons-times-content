Fairfax County residents have until February 21, 2025, to apply for two openings on the Facilities Planning Advisory Council, which helps shape long-term plans for school facilities.

The available seats represent the Braddock and Franconia magisterial districts. Both appointments expire June 30, 2025, and council members may be reappointed.

The 13-member council advises Fairfax County Public Schools staff and the School Board on comprehensive facilities planning. Its membership includes one representative from each Fairfax County magisterial district, three at-large representatives and one member from the City of Fairfax. Members are appointed to three-year terms.

Applicants must live in the district they seek to represent. FCPS particularly encourages people with experience in construction, engineering, architecture, land-use planning, development, demographics, government operations or financing to apply.

Applications and additional information about the council are available online. Completed applications must be emailed to [email protected] by the deadline.
