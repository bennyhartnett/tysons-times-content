Ray Zhang, a student at Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology, was named one of 40 finalists in the 2025 Regeneron Science Talent Search on Jan. 23.

Zhang advanced from a field of 300 scholars with a project examining biofilm composition in clinical isolates of the Fusarium fungus and developing a treatment that targets multiple pathways to inhibit fungal growth.

The Regeneron Science Talent Search, a Society for Science program, is a leading national science competition for students before college.

Each finalist will receive at least $25,000 and travel to Washington, D.C., in March for final judging. The students will also present their work to the public, meet prominent scientists and compete for additional awards, including a $250,000 top prize.

Winners are scheduled to be announced March 11.
