Fairfax County Public Schools athletes closed the 2024-25 winter season with a sweeping collection of Virginia High School League Class 6 honors. Five teams and 36 individual or relay entries won state championships, while five teams and 35 individual or relay entries finished as runners-up.

Lake Braddock Secondary School’s gymnastics team led the team champions by securing its fourth consecutive state title. South Lakes High School boys basketball repeated as champion for the second straight year.

Chantilly High School won the boys swim and dive championship, South County High School captured the girls indoor track and field title, and West Springfield High School claimed the boys indoor track and field crown.

## State champions

Gymnast Madi Bell of Lake Braddock won three titles: all-around, uneven parallel bars and balance beam.

The Class 6 wrestling champions were Michael Santos of Chantilly at 106 pounds, Carter Shin of Chantilly at 113, Tyler Dekraker of Chantilly at 138, Kross Cassidy of Fairfax at 165, Merritt Clington of Westfield at 175, Ethan Osburn of Hayfield at 190 and Harry Maltese of Fairfax at 215.

- Apria Smith of Chantilly in the girls triple jump

## State runners-up

Five FCPS teams placed second in Class 6 competition: Chantilly wrestling, Langley boys swim and dive, Robinson girls indoor track and field, South Lakes boys indoor track and field and Woodson boys basketball.

Gymnast Alexia Zakaryan of South County finished second on the uneven parallel bars.

The wrestling runners-up were Cristian Rojas of Hayfield at 106 pounds, Adam Husseini of West Springfield at 113, Troy Kang of Robinson at 120, Hana Coutts of Oakton in the girls 145-pound division, Aidan Sanders of South Lakes at 150 and Ashley Freeman of Mount Vernon at 215.
