Eleven Alexandria-area student-athletes were recognized as March Athletes of the Month during the Alexandria Sportsman’s Club’s March 19 meeting at the Old Dominion Boat Club.

Club President Ryan Fannon presented awards to:

- Chloe Lambert, lacrosse, St. Stephen’s & St. Agnes School
- Ella Stohs, swimming, Alexandria City High School
- Kendra Harris, lacrosse, Bishop Ireton High School
- Ella Nick, swimming, Bishop Ireton High School
- Joe Bucca, wrestling, Bishop Ireton High School
- Jarrel Long, basketball, Alexandria Youth Recreation Program
- Jack Gans, ice hockey, St. Stephen’s & St. Agnes School
- Tobias Damico-Lassman, swimming, Alexandria City High School
- Desmond Damico-Lassman, diving, Alexandria City High School
- Colin Pollock, basketball, St. Stephen’s & St. Agnes School
- Vincent Scheivert, lacrosse, Bishop Ireton High School

The gathering also included presentations highlighting high school spring sports teams.

The club’s next events include its annual Take Me Out to the Ballgame outing at 1:35 p.m. April 27, when the Washington Nationals play the New York Mets at Nationals Park.

The organization will then hold its 68th annual awards banquet May 28 at the Westin Alexandria Old Town. This year’s banquet will mark the debut of a scholarship supporting students pursuing vocational or technical education after high school.

Inspired by the late Alexandria philanthropist Marion Moon, the $2,500 scholarship will go to a high school senior in the City of Alexandria seeking trade-related skills or technical certifications.
