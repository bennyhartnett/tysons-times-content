Twenty-four Fairfax County Public Schools earned the 2025 Virginia Music Educators Association Blue Ribbon Award for musical excellence.

The Blue Ribbon is Virginia’s highest honor for school music programs. It recognizes exceptional performance across band, chorus, and orchestra.

To qualify, each school’s top ensemble in all three disciplines had to receive a final rating of Superior at a VMEA District Concert Performance Assessment.

The 2025 FCPS recipients are:

- Annandale High School
- Sandburg Middle School
- Woodson High School
- Chantilly High School
- Fairfax High School
- Frost Middle School
- Marshall High School
- Glasgow Middle School
- Hayfield Secondary School
- Herndon High School
- Madison High School
- Robinson Secondary School
- Justice High School
- Katherine Johnson Middle School

- West Springfield High School
- Westfield High School
