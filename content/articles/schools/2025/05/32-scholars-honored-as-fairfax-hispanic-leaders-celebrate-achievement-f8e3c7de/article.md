The Hispanic Leadership Alliance, an employee group within Fairfax County Public Schools, honored 32 high school seniors, adult learners and community leaders with scholarships on May 7, 2025.

Recipients are pursuing studies in fields including healthcare, education, engineering, psychology, law, public policy and cybersecurity. They were recognized for leadership and mentoring through their academic and community activities, as well as their commitment to continuing their education and helping others.

Liliana Velasco Fuentes received the Monroy-Lavalle Adult ESOL Scholarship. After a decades-long career in México, she is improving her English through the FCPS Family Literacy Program.

“Language can help me get a job in the future; a second language will allow me to help others in need; and I can grow personally and academically,” Fuentes said.

Crestwood Elementary School Principal Françoise Casablanca received the HLA Leadership Award. Students and staff celebrated her through video messages highlighting her compassionate leadership, kindness and work building a strong school community.
