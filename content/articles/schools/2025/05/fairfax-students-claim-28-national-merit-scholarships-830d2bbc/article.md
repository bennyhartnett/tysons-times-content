Twenty-eight Fairfax County Public Schools students have each won a $2,500 National Merit Scholarship, joining approximately 2,500 finalists selected nationwide for the awards in 2025.

The National Merit Scholarship Corporation evaluated candidates using their academic records, the difficulty of their coursework, grades, scores from two standardized tests, school and community leadership, personal essays and recommendations from high school officials.

The number of winners selected in each state is proportional to that state’s share of the country’s graduating high school seniors. The scholarship corporation, rather than the school district, chooses the recipients.
