Fairfax County Public Schools athletes closed the 2025 spring season with a sweeping collection of state honors, including five team championships and 17 individual or relay titles in Virginia High School League Class 6 competition.

Five FCPS teams captured VHSL championships, while five others finished as state runners-up. Athletes and relay teams also earned 20 runner-up finishes. FCPS schools added more honors in crew and rifle, which are not VHSL sports.

## Five teams claim VHSL championships

The 2025 Class 6 team champions were:

- South County High School — girls outdoor track and field
- Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology — girls tennis
- Langley High School — boys tennis
- West Potomac High School — boys lacrosse
- Herndon High School — boys soccer

The team runners-up were:

- West Springfield High School — boys outdoor track and field
- Oakton High School — girls tennis
- Madison High School — boys tennis
- Madison High School — girls lacrosse
- South County High School — girls soccer

## Robinson wins regional rifle championship

Robinson Secondary School captured the Potomac High School Rifle League Regional Championship, with Lake Braddock Secondary School finishing second.

Diana Leppert of Lake Braddock won the individual rifle title. Samuel Nickless of Robinson was the individual runner-up.
