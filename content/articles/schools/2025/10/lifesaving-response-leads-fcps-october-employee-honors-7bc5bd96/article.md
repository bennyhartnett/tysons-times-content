Two emergency responses that may have saved lives earned Officer Dani Khalife one of Fairfax County Public Schools’ top employee recognitions for October 2025.

At Westlawn Elementary School, Khalife encountered two people behaving unusually inside a parked vehicle. After recognizing that one appeared to be in medical distress, he provided aid, summoned medics and police, and monitored the second person until Officer Schmidt and local police arrived. The patient was taken to a hospital and was reportedly recovering.

During a separate school board meeting, Khalife found an unconscious person on a sidewalk. He called for medical assistance and worked with bystanders to revive the person before medics transported them to a hospital.

FCPS also selected monthly honorees from each of its six regions.

At Cunningham Park Elementary, Meg Burnside, Maldonado Montecinos and Mattie Edmonston surprised a student by joining a birthday drive-by featuring “cool cars,” the child’s special interest.

Haycock Elementary third-grade teacher Catie Bartczak was recognized for weekly kindness challenges that encouraged students to help at home, pick up trash and greet others without seeking credit.

Veronda Grimes, the administrative and principal’s assistant at Mount Vernon High School, was honored for handling urgent requests, supporting families and staff, coordinating schedules and coaching the school’s cheerleaders.

Union Mill Elementary family liaison Mindy Cucci received three nominations. Colleagues highlighted her work with Spanish-speaking and military-connected families, translation assistance, registration support, Purple Star programming and help for students fasting during Ramadan.

Wakefield Forest Elementary custodian Daud Ahmed was praised for keeping the school operating smoothly, responding efficiently to problems and reassuring children after accidental spills.

At Annandale High School, Cass Hersh and Jennifer Cory helped Bryant High School launch nearby Saturday IPFIT sessions by providing space, headphones and office access for individual speaking lessons.
