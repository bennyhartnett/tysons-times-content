Fairfax County Public Schools recognized 209 students for completing demanding AP Capstone requirements during the 2024-25 school year. Of those students, 187 earned the AP Capstone Diploma and 22 received the AP Seminar and Research Certificate.

The College Board program centers on two year-long courses, AP Seminar and AP Research. Students develop skills in critical thinking, academic research, collaboration, presenting findings and managing long-term projects. Assessments include research papers, multimedia presentations and defenses of students’ conclusions.

Students earn the AP Capstone Diploma by scoring 3 or higher in AP Seminar, AP Research and four other AP exams of their choice. The certificate requires scores of 3 or higher in AP Seminar and AP Research.
