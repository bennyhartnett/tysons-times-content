The National Merit Scholarship Program has held its annual academic competition since 1955, offering high school students recognition and opportunities for college scholarships. The National Merit Scholarship Corporation, a nonprofit that receives no government assistance, administers the program.

More than 1.3 million students enter each year by taking the Preliminary SAT/National Merit Scholarship Qualifying Test, which provides the competition’s initial screening. Students must also satisfy the program’s published entry and participation rules.

Students with signed opt-out forms on file are not included in the list.
