Twenty-six Fairfax County Public Schools students captured first-place honors at the 2026 Virginia Future Business Leaders of America State Leadership Conference in Reston.

More than 90 FCPS students finished among the top four in their events, qualifying them for the FBLA National Leadership Conference in San Antonio in June. Altogether, 196 students from county high schools competed across 72 categories, including accounting, 3-D animation, cybersecurity, computer problem solving and public speaking.

Two students also earned scholarships. Robinson Secondary School’s Sienna Wade received the L. Marguerite Crumley Scholarship, while Lake Braddock Secondary School’s Manas Puri received the Frank Manning Peele Scholarship.

FBLA, a career and technical student organization serving more than 200,000 members annually, prepares students for careers in business.
