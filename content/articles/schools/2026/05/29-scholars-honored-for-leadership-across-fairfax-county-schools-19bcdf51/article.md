The Hispanic Leadership Alliance recognized 29 Fairfax County Public Schools high school seniors, adult learners and community leaders with scholarships on Monday, May 4.

Recipients were selected for demonstrating leadership and mentorship through educational and community activities. Each plans to continue their education while contributing to their community.

Kailey Morales Campos, who received the West Springfield Hispanic Student Association Scholarship, said she had learned “the transformative power of creating spaces where people feel supported.”

Justice High School’s Jordin Ramos Galdamez, recipient of the Scholarship for Engineering, reflected on taking control of what comes next.

“My experiences have let me know that the past can’t be rewritten, and the future is my choice,” Galdamez said.

The alliance also presented its Leadership Award to FCPS Chief of Schools Dr. Geovanny Ponce. He was recognized for his vision, commitment and advocacy, including efforts to expand opportunities and support for students and families throughout the school division.

Ponce is scheduled to become superintendent of Champaign Unit 4 School District in Illinois on July 1.
