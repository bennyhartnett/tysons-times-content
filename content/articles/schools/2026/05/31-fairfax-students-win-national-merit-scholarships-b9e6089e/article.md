Thirty-one students representing 12 Fairfax County high schools have each won a $2,500 National Merit Scholarship.

The students are among approximately 2,500 National Merit finalists selected nationwide for scholarships in 2026. Their awards are financed by the National Merit Scholarship Corporation’s own funds.

Candidates were assessed on their academic records, the rigor of their coursework, grades and scores from two standardized tests. Evaluators also considered school and community leadership, personal essays and recommendations from high school officials.

The number of recipients selected in each state reflects that state’s share of graduating high school seniors nationwide. The National Merit Scholarship Corporation, rather than the school district, chooses the winners.
