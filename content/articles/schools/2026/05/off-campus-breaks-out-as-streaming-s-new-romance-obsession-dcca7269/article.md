In its first season, *Off Campus* has emerged as one of streaming’s most talked-about new dramas. Since premiering May 13 on Prime Video, the series has spread rapidly across social media—and a second season is already confirmed.

Adapted from Elle Kennedy’s popular book series, the show follows college hockey players juggling athletics, friendships, relationships and personal hardships.

Garrett Graham and Hannah Wells anchor the story. What begins as a straightforward tutoring arrangement gradually becomes a deeper relationship, with their chemistry driving the show’s combination of romance, humor and vulnerability.

The series pairs party scenes and playful banter with heavier subjects, including anxiety, trauma and self-confidence. Hannah continues to grapple with the effects of a sexual assault in high school, while Garrett carries the trauma of childhood abuse by his father. Their flaws and struggles give viewers a reason to become invested in their progress.

A cast of rising actors also helps distinguish the adaptation. Without many established Hollywood stars in the central roles, audiences can encounter the characters without strong preconceptions about the performers playing them.

Online enthusiasm has further expanded the show’s reach. Emotional clips, romantic moments and memorable one-liners have circulated widely on TikTok and Instagram, attracting viewers who had never read the books.

Rather than depending on constant twists or exaggerated characters, *Off Campus* builds its appeal around relationships and emotional development. Its blend of romance, comedy and serious personal struggles has turned the first season into a breakout—and positioned the growing franchise for more attention when it returns.
