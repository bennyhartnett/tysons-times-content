George Mason University sports fans will get a chance to meet the school’s coaches and athletics leaders during a free community event in downtown Fairfax.

The Northern Virginia’s Team Tour Coaches Caravan is scheduled for Saturday, June 27, from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. at Old Town Square, 10415 North St. An RSVP is required.

The all-ages gathering will bring together Mason Athletics coaches, staff, alumni, donors, families and fans. A coaches panel and question-and-answer session will feature updates, insights and stories from the university’s athletics program.

The evening will also include interactive activities, prizes, giveaways, Mason merchandise and opportunities to connect with fellow Patriots, community partners and local businesses.

The event is open to longtime supporters, alumni, season-ticket holders, parents, prospective students and anyone interested in the George Mason community.
